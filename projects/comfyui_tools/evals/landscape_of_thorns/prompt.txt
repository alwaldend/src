A panoramic grimdark painting: a forbidden nuclear-waste burial ground, a vast field of man-made stone thorns beneath a crushing storm. Monumental architecture, a brutally impaled, flesh-covered human corpse, the silence of a place built to outlast humanity.

Ground-level viewpoint, low horizon. Massive cropped roots frame the foreground; an off-center, forked thorn towers above the skyline. Broad stone bases taper continuously into impossibly tall, leaning needle-point stems. A few long, blade-like side-spurs sweep outward from thick, seamless joints. Smaller angular thorns interlock in dense, irregular ranks to the horizon. Their crossing diagonals close in like a trap. These are weathered charcoal-gray monuments with faceted edges and chipped tips, rooted solidly in ash and broken stone.

On one side-spur in the nearer middle distance hangs a dead adult human body, small beside the monument but unmistakably a flesh-covered corpse. The upward-slanting stone pierces its back; a long, blood-darkened point emerges through a ragged wound in the chest. Gray-green leathery skin still covers the torso, abdomen, arms and legs, with dark mottling and torn flesh around the wound. Its head lolls sideways: a sunken human face with intact cheeks, shriveled lips, a slack open mouth and clouded eyes beneath half-closed lids. Both arms dangle heavily below the pierced torso; the legs hang at broken, uneven angles. Shredded burial rags snag on the stone and trail in the wind. Harsh rim light picks out the damaged chest, dead face and limp fingers against an opening in the haze. A grotesque, abandoned corpse with substantial flesh and real weight, suspended on a visibly connected stone spur.

The thorn field commands the first glance; the corpse delivers a shocking second reading. Leave a narrow interval of pale mist behind the body so its damaged silhouette remains legible. Vast empty ground and receding ranks make this single death feel pitilessly insignificant.

An almost-black storm presses down on a thin, sickly ivory light break. Raking light cuts across the stone and casts long, tangled shadows toward the viewer. Charcoal, bruised olive and cold ash; sparse bone-white accents and dried oxblood confined to the body. Foreground masses stay dark and sharp; distant ranks dissolve into cold haze. Oppressive scale, imminent weather, no refuge.

Painterly digital fine art with the gravity of a monumental oil painting. Bold designed shapes, asymmetrical balance, selective impasto on lit stone, scumbled cloud banks, lost edges in shadow. Resolve a few crucial forms; subordinate surface detail to light, silhouette and atmosphere.

Borderless artwork without lettering, captions, signatures or watermarks.
