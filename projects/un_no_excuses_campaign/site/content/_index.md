This project reconstructs the supplied army spot as an editable Blender scene
and rendered animation. The current stylized remake follows the source's shot
sequence and soundtrack, while character likeness and motion remain approximate.

## Features

- Editable characters, settings, cameras, and animation
- Rendered video with the supplied soundtrack
- Source comparison and verification records
