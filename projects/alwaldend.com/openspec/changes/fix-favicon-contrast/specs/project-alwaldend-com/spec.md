## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Browser tab icon contrast

The site SHALL serve the S8 favicon with fixed contrasting artwork and
background colors under a content-fingerprinted URL. The header logo SHALL
retain its transparent background and adaptive foreground.

#### Scenario: Display the favicon in a browser tab

- **WHEN** a browser displays the favicon against a light or dark tab
- **THEN** the S8 artwork remains legible against the favicon’s own background
- **AND** the icon’s colors remain consistent across color preferences
