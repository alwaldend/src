## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Browser tab icon contrast

The site SHALL publish the S8 favicon with a white fill and a thin charcoal
outline on a transparent background. The same artwork SHALL appear for both
browser color preferences. The URL SHALL be fingerprinted from the icon
contents. The header logo SHALL retain its adaptive foreground.

#### Scenario: Display the favicon on light and dark tabs

- **WHEN** the browser displays the favicon at 16 by 16 pixels on a light or
  dark tab
- **THEN** the S8 silhouette and the gaps in its spiral remain distinguishable
