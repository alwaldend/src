## Why

The outlined S8 monogram looks crowded at mobile tab size. The outline narrows
the open spaces between turns and makes the mark resemble several nested outlines.

## What Changes

- Use the original S8 monogram in charcoal on a rounded white tile for browser tabs.
- Preserve the adaptive colors in the header.
- Fingerprint the favicon URL from the published artwork.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `project-alwaldend-com` covers S8 legibility in the browser tab.

## Impact

The site publishes an updated SVG favicon across its shared page layouts.
