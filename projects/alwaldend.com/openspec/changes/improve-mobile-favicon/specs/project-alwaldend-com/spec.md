## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Browser tab icon contrast

The site SHALL publish the canonical S8 artwork in charcoal on a rounded
white tile. The same artwork SHALL appear for both browser color preferences.
The URL SHALL be fingerprinted from the icon contents. The header logo SHALL
retain its adaptive foreground.

#### Scenario: Display the favicon in the mobile tab strip

- **WHEN** the browser displays the favicon at 16 by 16 pixels on a light or
  dark tab
- **THEN** the S8 silhouette and the gaps in its spiral remain distinguishable
