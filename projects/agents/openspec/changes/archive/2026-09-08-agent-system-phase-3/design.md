## Decisions

### Deterministic advisory planning

ImpactPlan resolves profiles, capabilities, allowed and forbidden effects, narrow changed and reverse-affected targets, minimum checks, coverage gaps, cost, escalation, and applicable evidence from canonical inputs.

### Admission at provider boundaries

Read and hermetic computation retain a cheap path. Remote effects follow prepare, validate, authorize, execute, and verify, with refusal on stale state, wrong environment, missing budgets, and unknown contracts.

### Immutable validation and separate assertions

ValidationSet binds candidate, checks, arguments, scope, toolchain and policy identities, result, coverage, bounds, and limitations. EvidenceAssertion applies immutable validation to a criterion revision; tree-bound and commit-bound applicability remain distinct.

### Resumable structured audits

Full-repository reports are incremental JSON with generated Markdown, selectors, exact input binding, target counts, and resume only on identical inputs. Raw logs remain task-local and bounded.
