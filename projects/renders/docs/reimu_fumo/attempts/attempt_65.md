## 65A — low-resolution sculptable cages

tails exactly and passed isolation and basic topology checks. Their first
packet nevertheless reads as inflated mouse-ear pads in front, paddles in both
obliques, one thick vertical petal in side, and split hollow shells in rear.
The trim is piping-like and the center is a dark cleft rather than a gathered
fabric root. The branch stopped without tuning.

## 65B — explicit front, turn, and return leaf

This final branch tested the strongest remaining bow hypothesis: separate
front and return paths joined by a rounded outer fold, with a finite gathered
throat and path-derived frames. Both loop meshes are single closed components,
the legacy tails remain fingerprint-exact, and the material upper span is
`1.498092 Wh`, inside the corrected target. Candidate SHA-256 is
`0566b603c87c3da563cf7622f80e11fd313d0c8eb5dd93e2590e8f1005826b72`.

The fixed pixels reject the construction absolutely. Front reads as two tall
rectangular cat-ear cards with a center notch. Both obliques expose hard
upright slabs. Exact side becomes one thick rectangular fin with a rolled rim,
not a readable outgoing/turn/return S-fold. Rear exposes separated root strips
and rigid legacy tails rather than one compressed sewn fan.

## 65D — adversarial whole-process review

The review compares Attempts 8–11, 42, 44–50, and 62–65 against the complete
plush goal rather than selecting a least-bad local candidate. It finds that the
same missing returned depth and broad gathered root survives thin, volumetric,
simulated, swept, unified, split-panel, low-resolution cage, and dense-relief
families. Several branches pass envelope, topology, or stability while scoring
only `1–4/10` for construction or side read. The visible bow also depends on a
crown that remains a rejected helmet.

- [Adversarial strategy review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_065_parallel/STRATEGY_REVIEW.md)

**Primary verdict:** accept the strategy reset. Close visible bow work after
the already-running 65B packet. Make the unified head/hair/crown receiver the
primary Attempt 66 lane and the compact torso/seat/skirt/contact receiver a
disjoint parallel lane. Reopen the bow only after the crown passes.

## Result

No Attempt 65 candidate is a new parent. The useful result is negative but
actionable: the upper-bow representation family is no longer allowed to absorb
the main iteration budget, and its crown dependency is restored. Attempt 66
now tests the two coupled macro receivers responsible for the largest complete-
subject failures.
