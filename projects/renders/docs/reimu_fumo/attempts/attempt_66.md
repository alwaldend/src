## 66H — head, hair, and crown receiver

The lane replaced the exact audited fifteen-object head/hair boundary, hid the
finished bow for an unobstructed crown gate, and preserved the seven face/eye
registration objects plus the complete body. It built a shallow front/rear/
gusset cushion, one closed low-fill crown/fringe/temple/rear mantle, two cheek
locks, and four rear pockets. Candidate SHA-256 is
`2f0ee05ee60b6b66b3015d524a93c26424e303e4ead48e0a843f9b18408f3751`.

The frontal width allocation is directionally closer, but the fixed views
reject the representation. The cushion and mantle form a deep rounded box or
helmet; the beige face becomes a thin plaque; a sharply bounded bald crown
patch remains; rear pockets become regular petals; and narrow strands read as
detached cards. Independent review scores silhouette `3/10`, construction
`2/10`, contact `3/10`, and medium read `2/10`. One bounded correction cannot
repair the independent box, plaque, bald, and panel-continuity failures.

## 66L — torso, seat, skirt, and contact receiver

The lane hid the rejected lower garment system while preserving head, bow,
sleeves, feet, and leg roots. A flat-bottom radial seat/torso receiver, doubled
front and rear lap panels, and provisional hem envelopes pass every written
mechanical gate: visible stack `0.577273 Wh`, front skirt `0.915103 Wh`, hem
`1.064394 Wh`, side footprint `1.133121 Wh`, support/floor separation
`0.002652 Wh`, closed meshes, exact frozen context, and zero new foot
intersections. Candidate SHA-256 is
`5be3dbfd0dad9d5983a2b3f0f06527be9c1546e9f4b90170d878e4b1fe8155e5`.

The pixels expose why those gates are insufficient. Front and rear become a
rigid table/tent; exact side is a straight ramp/cape with a long white floor
rail; obliques are hard shelves; the visible panels do not express the hidden
ground support; and a large background-colored gap disconnects the head and
sleeves. Blind scores are silhouette `3/10`, construction `2/10`, contact
`1/10`, and medium `2/10`.

## Result and next action

Attempt 66 does not advance the working parent. It does convert two vague
complaints into exact replacement scopes, calibrated contracts, and decisive
representation failures. Attempt 67 performs one materially different
topology cycle per lane on fresh parent copies. If the same helmet/panel-
continuity or cone/ramp/contact complaint survives its fixed-view packet, that
representation closes instead of receiving a third tuning cycle.
