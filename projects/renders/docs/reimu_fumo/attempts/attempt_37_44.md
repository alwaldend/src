# Reimu Fumo attempts 37–44

[Back to attempt index](README.md) | [Back to goal](../README.md)

These attempts changed the workflow from repeated whole-model rebuilds to
reference-calibrated, immutable-copy module work. A technically clean or
relatively better candidate is still recorded as rejected when its pixels do
not read as the supplied sewn plush.

## Attempt 37 — representation resets

Five-face, layered-shell, core/opening, and whole-plush macro constructions
were tested. All visible candidates were rejected: they became boxes, helmets,
plates, cards, or stacked slabs despite valid topology and several matched
landmarks. See the
[layered-head sheet](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_037_layered_head_a/review_sheet.png),
[core-shell sheet](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_037_sculpt_core_b/review_sheet.png),
and [macro review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_037_macro_sculpt/absolute_review.md).

The result retired uniform-depth extrusions and analytic outer envelopes as a
substitute for manufactured plush form.

## Attempt 38 — constructed sculpt

Soft cores plus separate curved panels replaced thin cards, but the complete
plush still read as a rounded bench, spherical bald head, spikes, and inflated
lobes. Overall likeness was scored `2/10`. See the
[front render](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_038_constructed_sculpt/renders/clay_front.png)
and [blind review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_038_constructed_sculpt/blind_review.md).

Generic soft primitives were therefore rejected as another representation,
not accepted merely because they were smoother.

## Attempt 39 — parallel module variants

Independent head/hair, bow, and body/garment candidates were built. None
passed: the head remained a rounded helmet, bow an upright sheet assembly, and
garment a taut symmetric bell. See the
[head review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_039_head_hair_variant/direct_sculpt/blind_review.md),
[bow sheet](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_039_bow_variant/contact_sheet.png),
and [garment scorecard](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_039_body_garment_variant/scorecard.md).

The useful survivor was methodological: independent views and modules expose
wrong construction earlier than another assembled rebuild.

## Attempt 40 — art-directed cages

Sectioned volumes and sewn-pillow controls remained oversized, detached, and
weak in side/rear views. No acceptance record exists. See the
[front](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_040_artdirected_cages/renders/material_front.png),
[side](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_040_artdirected_cages/renders/material_side_left.png),
and [rear](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_040_artdirected_cages/renders/material_rear.png).

## Attempt 41 — manual maquette

A stable editable maquette and calibrated cameras improved iteration, but the
assembled result still used disconnected smooth primitives. The head review
rejected its molded helmet, rectangular rear hair, boxy opening, and inflated
bow; the later complete model scored `3/10` likeness and `1/10` construction.
See the [Stage E review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_041_manual_head_maquette/stage_e/blind_review.md)
and [final fixed views](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_041_manual_head_maquette/stage_j/full_front.png).

The maquette became an experimental source, never an approved asset.

## Attempt 42 — isolated module studies

Face, hair, garment, bow, cloth, material, and rig-interface studies were
isolated. Every complete visible module was rejected. The only narrow visual
direction worth retaining was shallow surface-relative eye applique at the
correct centers; the rig work established an interface plan but intentionally
did not freeze rejected geometry. See the
[face review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_042_face_module/REVIEW.md),
[garment review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_042_garment_module/REVIEW.md),
[material review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_042_material_module/BLIND_REVIEW.md),
and [rig plan](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_042_rig_design/PLAN.md).

## Attempt 43 — integrated relative improvements

The best Attempt 42 directions were combined. The front became more
recognizable, but the shared construction errors survived: helmet hair,
canopy/fin bow, cone sleeves, frustum skirt, ball feet, and weak seated
contact. The integrated candidate scored `4/10` overall likeness and `2/10`
for construction and medium. See the
[blind review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_043_integrated_modules/blind_review_stage_b.md)
and [neutral front](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_043_integrated_modules/stage_c_neutral/front.png).

Attempt 43 is the immutable copy source for bounded tests, not an approval
baseline.

## Attempt 44 — process and dependency reset

The session established a measured all-reference construction contract, a
calibrated two-view/five-view render pipeline, a hardened silhouette evaluator,
implementation-blind review, and an animatable rig/API prototype. The visible
bow, skirt, head envelope/FFD, interactive sculpt, and first sleeve forms were
all rejected. Face Cycle 7 and a later panel sleeve were directional only.

The arXiv-guided FFD experiment was killed after worsening head landmark RMS by
about twelve times. This confirmed that the active bottleneck is visible sewn
construction, not hidden-envelope optimization or a missing reconstruction
paper.

## Result and next dependency

No whole-model visual asset passed. The reusable outputs are process and
technical infrastructure plus a few receiver-dependent subparts. Work now
proceeds in dependency order: unified visible hair; compact seated lower stack;
bow root; sleeve/upper-body receiver; face reprojection; materials; then a
one-writer integrated five-view gate and rig packaging.
