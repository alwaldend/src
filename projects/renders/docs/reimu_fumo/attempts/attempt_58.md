# Attempt 58 — layered-head representation bake-off

[Back to attempt index](README.md) · [Back to goal](../README.md)

## Failure and frozen hypothesis

Attempt 57 proved that a brown stuffed core with a shallow beige face can pass
scalar extent gates while still reading as a helmet, plaque, hard `M/W`
fringe, and rear slab. Attempt 58 therefore tested visible layer ownership
rather than tuning that rejected core.

The [source-only construction audit](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_058_layered_head/reference_construction_audit.md)
used the canonical 25 cm front, canonical turn, both physical photographs,
and both supplied GIFs. It rejected a literal brown-core/face-patch model and
found the strongest directly visible hierarchy to be:

1. a volume-bearing structural beige face;
2. a close-fitted brown crown/fringe with a traceable free edge; and
3. separate overlapping temple and rear leaves.

The hidden crown/back substrate remains unproved by any supplied pixel. The
audit ranked the named source hypotheses `B > C > A`, but recommended a
bounded hybrid that does not invent the hidden substrate.

The frozen [attempt contract](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_058_layered_head/ATTEMPT.md)
and [representation decision](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_058_layered_head/DECISION.md)
authorized three independent factory-startup coupons: a conforming surface
cap, a composite shell, and image-keyed panels. Each had to supply fixed front,
three-quarter, and true-side beauty and form evidence, a source comparison,
an immutable blend hash, reopen validation, timings, and first-pixel review.
Any helmet, egg, box, mattress, plaque, rigid slab, disconnected card, broken
side depth, or clipped proof-lighting veto rejected the representation.

## Anonymous comparison and absolute verdict

Exact render hashes map the anonymous rows as `X = B`, `Y = C`, and `Z = A`.
The [beauty matrix](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_058_layered_head/blind_comparison/beauty_matrix.png),
[form matrix](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_058_layered_head/blind_comparison/form_matrix.png),
and [implementation-blind review](../../../../../out/reimu_fumo_attempt_058_layered_head/blind_comparison/BLIND_REVIEW.md)
ranked `X > Z > Y`, while rejecting all three. A second implementation-blind
pixel audit ranked the same rows `Z > Y > X`, also rejecting all three. The
disagreement makes the relative winner unstable; no coupon is a defensible
"least bad" parent.

The stable cross-review result is **all reject**:

| Criterion                              | A    | B    | C    |
| -------------------------------------- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Reopen, hash, and technical hygiene    | pass | pass | pass |
| Front/side/three-quarter macro form    | fail | fail | fail |
| Face/head integration and layer order  | fail | fail | fail |
| Manufactured plush construction        | fail | fail | fail |
| Crown/fringe identity                  | fail | fail | fail |
| Side/rear attachment and leaf behavior | fail | fail | fail |
| Absolute `8/10`, no-major-defect gate  | fail | fail | fail |

No candidate was promoted, averaged, or integrated. The protected standalone
Reimu and Sisyphus blends were not modified.

## Progress, approach, and process audit

- No model acceptance criterion measurably improved. The coupons improved
  failure attribution only: surface caps become helmets, composite lofts
  become boxes, and independently keyed panels become armor or curtains.
- Numeric extent, closed-manifold topology, source-keyed landmarks, and fast
  mesh construction again failed to predict visible plush construction.
- A58's approximately two-minute render packets were not the primary
  bottleneck; all three meshes were generated in under half a second. The
  critical failure was choosing degrees of freedom that do not correspond to
  sewing, stuffing equilibrium, soft seam roots, or fabric compression.
- The anonymous packet has comparison confounds: A uses corrected beauty with
  first-state form, B's lighting clips, and no row includes a rear view. These
  weaken fine ranking but cannot rescue any absolute veto.
- Retire all three A58 meshes, dimensions, and layer stacks. A successor may
  retain only the source-derived visible ordering and fixed cross-view gates.
  It must change method toward a genuinely pattern/seam-driven, shallow
  cushion construction with compression-permitting stuffing behavior, then
  stop on its first helmet, mattress, box, plaque, card, floating-root, or
  context-regression evidence.
