## Attempt 17 — registered coupled head-and-hair target

**Candidate:** planned temporary registered target packet under
`out/reimu_fumo_attempt_017_coupled_head_target/`; no Blender candidate,
topology, or reusable asset is permitted in this attempt.

**Failure targeted:** Attempts 15 and 16 froze precise hidden-cushion geometry
that the references do not expose. Attempt 16's invented target was internally
incompatible and failed before modeling.

**Hypothesis:** The coupled beige support and brown hair cap/fringe/locks have
observable front boundaries and recognizable construction. Registering those
boundaries directly over the physical source can supply a truthful sculpt
target while leaving hidden support regions as explicit uncertainty instead of
optimizing them toward another invented primitive.

**Plan written before implementation:**

1. Create no Blender file, mesh, material, or driver. Use only temporary local
   image processing and registered vector evidence. A failed guide ends this
   attempt before modeling.
2. Freeze the physical-front source as the primary controller at SHA-256
   `f8c7d0f9911dbff1ef7f5d75601f9b10825015aecb367381971c076a5a3e7b51`.
   Its outer head/hair box is `(143,104)–(332,298)`, with `Wh=189 px`.
   Register that box to `500 px` width on a `640 px` square canvas, align its
   horizontal center and top, and record the complete affine transform.
3. Trace the observable physical-front outer head/hair contour through these
   source pixels in clockwise order:
   `(236,104)`, `(278,108)`, `(311,136)`, `(327,172)`, `(331,211)`,
   `(326,253)`, `(320,292)`, `(290,299)`, `(236,298)`, `(181,299)`,

   `(205,106)`. Use a smooth curve through every point; preserve a visible

4. Trace the visible beige face opening and controlling fringe in source pixels

5. The clean front cross-check controls graphic clarity, not outer stuffing.

8. Hard-reject rounded box, mattress, egg, sphere, helmet, generic bob haircut,

9. A PASS freezes only the coupled front target and hidden-view uncertainty

   It does not approve geometry or relax the final reusable-model gate.

**Structural difference from Attempt 16:** every precise curve is drawn over
an observable source boundary; hidden cushion and side surfaces remain bands;
the target contains the identity-bearing hair silhouette that the user can
actually compare; and a failed target costs no Blender execution.

**Result:** Terminal target rejection. The source-and-trace manifest SHA-256
was `ef18d6cb3bcc174db406422ac2065b9e30a1ee29fa76fe602e7d36de5cfdb566`;
the review packet SHA-256 was
`b6c19edfb977ad183cabf9eb84f0e5eaf6c852f58f966129a35c6d6ccceb9e41`;
and the implementation-blind review SHA-256 was
`b3ab03b5eee0588725dc40a5699cafe386de3816ecaa85d8f20d2d36ce34571a`.
No Blender driver, mesh, or candidate was created.

The primary blind review scored front silhouette `6.3/10`, face-opening and
fringe construction `5.2/10`, softness potential `5.4/10`, registration
legibility `8.0/10`, and reported a major failure. The trace closed one brown
support ring beneath the chin through the collar region instead of modeling an
open neck/collar transition, a separate crown/fringe panel, and independently
terminating side locks. It also made the lower opening too broad, displaced the
left forehead apex, and made the right lobe too tall and geometric.

A second context-light audit scored registration `5/10` and plush construction
`3/10`. It measured a roughly `5% Wh` central-fringe displacement and a larger
right-lobe mismatch, and found a flat bottom rail, parallel sides, concentric
hard edging, uniform fill, and nested graphic slabs. The broad central point
avoided a thin spike but did not overcome the generic helmet construction.

**Criterion results:** Source-registration legibility alone passes its local
threshold. Character recognition, coupled construction, softness potential,
all model criteria, reuse, and animation remain failed or unverified.

**Decision:** Reject the single closed coupled trace. Do not convert it to
geometry. Preserve its registered overlay as evidence that an accurate outer
box is insufficient when the fabric pieces are topologically wrong.

### Progress and approach audit after attempt 17

- **Improved:** the raw photograph and overlay made the exact construction
  error visible before modeling, and the front outer boundary was closer than
  the invented Attempt 16 guide.
- **Regressed or unchanged:** the standalone trace still reads as a generic
  helmet, and no Blender pixel improved.
- **Absolute result:** `6.3/10` silhouette with `3/10` plush construction is a
  failed target, not a modeling base.
- **Invalid approach:** one nested brown region cannot represent a Fumo hair
  assembly. It turns crown, fringe, side locks, rear hair, and the open collar
  gap into one continuous ring even when its outside edge follows the photo.
- **Evidence against continuing:** a more exact spline would retain the wrong
  panel ownership. The failure is topology and layering, not point density.
- **Highest-leverage unresolved problem:** construct the visible front crown
  and fringe as one thin curved fabric panel, construct left and right locks as
  separate padded panels, and leave the beige support open at the collar.
- **Approach decision:** stop generating a standalone target silhouette.
  Attempt 18 places the raw registered source beside Blender and builds the
  actual observed pieces directly. Its first gate is front-only and small; rear
  hair, bow, body, graphics, and final material remain excluded.
