# A75 C0 implementation-blind face-carrier review

## Review isolation

The initial verdict was made from pixels only, before reading the A75 builder,
topology proposal, integration notes, build report, or earlier verdict on the
salvaged object. The review compared:

- the protected rung-003 front and both three-quarter renders;
- the immutable A75 C0 front and both three-quarter renders plus its cushion
  ID passes;
- the canonical 25 cm front and the complete canonical-turn contact sheet;
- the physical front and oblique-side photographs; and
- the clean front, older turn, and sofa evidence in their documented
  supporting roles.

Only the exposed beige face-carrier region is scored as the changed module.
The complete subject is checked only for regressions.

## Blind pixel verdict

**Reject A75 C0 and prefer the protected rung-003 baseline.** C0 is not an
unambiguous improvement in the front or either three-quarter view. The
baseline is too flat and still not approval quality, but its face remains a
quieter, broader fabric plane. C0 turns the lower face into a small spherical
mask or muzzle, introduces a conspicuous dark cheek-to-chin ring, and weakens
the face/hair/collar contacts.

| Affected-region category | Rung 003 | A75 C0 | Pixel-based reason |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Reference-variant identity | 5/10 | **4/10** | C0 narrows and rounds the visible face into a doll-like ball; the references show a broad, calm face plane with only a soft lower roll. |
| Constructed-plush read | 4/10 | **3/10** | The baseline is plaque-flat, but C0 overcorrects into an evenly inflated oval/muzzle rather than a shallow stuffed panel. |
| Contact and occlusion | 5/10 | **3/10** | C0 creates a dark perimeter/cavity under both eyes and along the chin, harder jaw/collar tangencies, and detached beige islands in both three-quarter ID passes. |

The affected-region gate required all three C0 scores to reach at least 6/10
and required a clear A/B preference. It meets neither condition.

## Decisive visible defects

1. **Wrong normal field and volume allocation.** The reference face is quiet
   and nearly planar through the eyes and mouth, with curvature increasing
   mainly through the lower cheek and underside. C0 is already strongly
   convex at eye level and rounds continuously into a near-circular lower
   face. It reads as a face ball, muzzle, or beard-shaped insert.
2. **The visible face aperture becomes too small and too round.** In front,
   C0 pulls both cheek edges inward and makes the eyes dominate the opening.
   The source has broader lower-middle cheeks and a broad shallow chin arc,
   not a small oval bounded by a dark ring.
3. **The dark cheek-to-chin ring is a categorical contact failure.** It is
   visible from the front and grows stronger in both three-quarter views. It
   reads as a cavity, embedded mask, or unsupported air gap rather than soft
   hair-over-face compression.
4. **Three-quarter exposure is too bulbous.** The references collapse toward
   a narrow beige crescent as the plush turns. C0 instead exposes a rounded
   side of the face below the eyes, so the carrier projects forward as a
   separate oval volume.
5. **The component pass exposes disconnected beige islands.** Both
   three-quarter cushion masks contain a small detached visible patch near the
   lower side/hair/collar junction. Even without proving geometric
   intersection, that projection is an unacceptable leakage/occlusion result.

The bow, body, skirt, sleeves, feet, and outer hair silhouette show no obvious
new macro change; preserving them does not rescue the changed face region.
The new face is the most attention-drawing regression in the whole-plush
views.

## Bounded-correction decision

**Do not spend the allowed bounded depth-course correction on C0. Reset this
candidate representation.** The failure is not merely excessive hidden
depth: the visible front aperture, visible surface normals, cheek/chin course,
and contact projection are all wrong. Correcting them would require
reauthoring the visible front field, not a bounded unobserved-depth or stuffing
tension adjustment.

The post-verdict implementation reveal reinforces that decision:

- C0 directly reuses the A66 face cushion rather than implementing the
  purpose-designed A75 face field;
- the reused base is `242` vertices / `280` polygons, not the documented A75
  `226`-vertex / `224`-quad chart; and
- A66's terminal record explicitly retired this cushion family after its face
  read as a hard plaque and its coupled head read as a rounded box/helmet.

Discard C0, preserve rung 003, and keep the narrow A75 interface only for a
fresh purpose-built carrier with a broad low-curvature eye/mouth plateau, a
late soft cheek/chin roll, and hidden transitions that do not produce side
islands. If that new representation cannot keep the frozen appliques seated
while beating rung 003 in all three controlling views, close the one-object
module as an interface failure rather than tuning it again.

## Decision

- A/B preference: **rung 003** in front and both three-quarter views.
- A75 C0 affected-region scores: **identity 4/10, construction 3/10,
  contact 3/10**.
- Major visible failure: **yes**.
- Categorical vetoes: **face-ball/muzzle read, dark perimeter/cavity, and
  detached beige side islands**.
- Next action: **reset C0 representation; no bounded depth-course correction**.
