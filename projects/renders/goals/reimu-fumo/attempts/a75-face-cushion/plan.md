## Stable defect and process correction

A74 failed before rear construction: its one-sided rectangular face support
became the dominant visible object, while hiding every legacy hair owner made
the rear failure inevitable. A75 does not rebuild the whole head. It isolates
the well-defined beige stuffed cushion and retains the protected complete
hair, bow, face appliques, body, clothing, cameras, and lights as integration
context.

## Hypothesis

Replacing only `Head_Cushion_Manual_Target` with a soft, rounded, seam-aware
face cushion can improve the plush read and facial carrier without requiring
an atomic hair rebuild. The new cushion must remain subordinate to the
existing hair silhouette. If the old hair geometry makes an improved cushion
impossible to judge or forces the new cushion back into the old helmet/egg
shape, the module interface is invalid and A75 returns early before Blender.
