## Whole-process review

A69--A72 made validation faster and more honest but did not improve the
protected whole model. Their shared failure is that reference observations
live in prose while vertices come from legacy forms, arbitrary primitives, or
guessed global formulas. Receiver-only review also hides the known
receiver/hair/root incompatibility. The next work unit therefore binds one
explicit profile table to both generated geometry and numeric preflight, then
tests the smallest visible hair assembly in whole-subject context.

Do not optimize render startup, materials, fibers, rigging, or microdetail in
this attempt. Those are not the current bottleneck.

## Decision review

The strongest objection to another analytic loft is that it can create a
cleaner generic cushion without making the visible Fumo better. The strongest
objection to a whole-head rebuild is the opposite: too many coupled unknowns
would prevent diagnosis and repeat the one-shot failures. Retuning A72 cannot
separate its crown, face, rear maximum, or underside. Synthetic native sculpt
support failed twice.

Verdict: proceed with a bounded two-gate coupon. P0 tests a genuinely new,
reference-bounded asymmetric profile cage. P1 is mandatory before promotion
and derives only a close-fitted crown/temple mantle and separate lower rear
leaves from the same cage. A receiver-only pass is evidence, not a usable rung.

## Controlling evidence

- The canonical 1000 px front controls exact-variant width, crown, frontal
  proportions, and 25 cm scale.
- All 30 canonical turn frames control side/rear envelope, depth distribution,
  layer order, and free rear-hair reserve. Selected frames are merely review
  witnesses and do not replace the sequence.
- Physical front and side control stuffing, shallow face construction, panel
  thickness, and drape. Clean front controls graphic aperture cross-checks.
- Older turn and sofa GIFs veto implausible transitions and rigid construction
  but do not override the canonical pair dimensionally.
- A69--A72 packets are negative evidence for helmet roots, independent cards,
  ineffective strokes, rounded mattresses, eggs, and pole closures.

The hidden receiver is not directly measurable in photographs. Profile rows
are reversible construction hypotheses inside frozen outer-envelope bands;
they may not be presented as traced hidden seams.

## Bounded plan

1. Record source-frame landmarks and uncertainty before geometry. Store 13
   independent height stations for half-width, face reach, rear reach, and the
   visible outer-hair envelope. The builder and preflight consume the same
   table; no duplicated prose constants are allowed.
2. Static preflight must reject out-of-band width/height/depth, a rear maximum
   outside `t=.64--.73`, lower-rear undercut outside `.05--.08 Wh`, a face-zone
   reach variation above `.010 Wh`, any single-pole cap, or unexpected profile
   reversal before Blender starts.
3. Generate one low-density all-quad cage at `Wh=132 mm`, with independent
   front/rear/width curves, explicit C1 azimuthal anchors, broad crown and
   underside cap patches, and named longitudinal attachment loops. Do not use
   signed powers, superellipsoids, Icospheres, or geometry from A72.
4. P0 receiver gate: render front, both profiles, rear, both three-quarters,
   top, and bottom in neutral clay. Reopen and render with pinned Blender 5.2.1.
   Require numeric topology gates and implementation-blind macro-silhouette
   score at least 6/10. Reset immediately on egg, mattress, rounded box, pole
   pinch, slab, shield, low rear maximum, or missing nape undercut.
5. If P0 passes, derive a minimal visible brown crown/temple mantle from the
   same cage plus separate free lower rear-leaf proxies. Transfer roots using
   surface-relative bands rather than frozen world coordinates; never add a
   bridge to reach incompatible legacy roots.
6. P1 integration gate: render the minimal assembly in the frozen rung-003
   subject context from front, both profiles, rear, and both three-quarters.
   Every view packet includes repository-relative reference paths, hashes,
   side-by-side witnesses, and registered silhouette overlays or edge
   differences. Compare against both references and rung 003.
7. Pass only if the whole-subject coupon is preferred over rung 003 without a
   front identity regression, critical visible contour errors are at most
   `.05 Wh`, macro silhouette is at least 6/10, constructed-plush read is at
   least 5/10, and there is no helmet, card, bald gap, floating root, exposed
   bridge, or receiver protrusion. A receiver that passes alone but fails P1
   is not promoted.

## Initial shared profile contract

- `Wh=132 mm`; evaluated height `.880 Wh`; evaluated maximum receiver depth
  `.800 Wh` inside the frozen `.77--.85 Wh` band.
- Thirteen terminal/interior rings use the explicit table in the A73 design
  evidence; rear reach peaks at `t=.69`, front reach is nearly fixed from
  `t=.28--.69`, and the lower rear is `.065 Wh` forward of the maximum.
- Thirty-two perimeter samples use explicit front-center, front-shoulder,
  temple, rear-shoulder, and rear-center anchors rather than one section
  exponent.
- Crown and underside close through broad all-quad patches with ordinary
  center valence, no triangle fans, no axis pole, and no n-gons.
- The visible hair mantle is a surface-relative derived layer. Independent
  rear leaves own the reserved posterior silhouette; the receiver may not be
  inflated to fill it.
