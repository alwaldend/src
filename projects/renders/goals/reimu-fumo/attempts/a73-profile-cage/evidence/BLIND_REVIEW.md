# Implementation-blind receiver review

## Verdict

**REJECT — 4/10 for receiver macro silhouette.**

The score is below the required 6/10 threshold, and the form is not yet
directionally viable for a mandatory visible hair coupon. The candidate has
clean, broad closures and adequate gross rear volume, but its dominant read is
a smooth rounded box in front/rear/top views and a tall egg in side views.
Those are explicit gate failures, not small finish issues.

## Review isolation and scope

This judgment was made from the eight supplied candidate renders, the
canonical front, the supplied turn frames, and the supporting physical and
clean reference images. No scripts, parameters, topology, implementation
diagnostics, prior candidate, or author verdict were consulted. Only the
receiver rest-surface macro form was judged; missing identity, hair, and body
were excluded.

## Gate findings

| Criterion | Finding |
|---|---|
| Broad stuffed support | **Partial.** The mass is broad in front and rear, but reads as uniformly inflated rather than as a deliberately shaped stuffed support. |
| Shallow face zone | **Fail.** The side silhouette exposes a tall, near-vertical front wall. The front does not resolve as a localized shallow face-rest zone distinct from crown, cheek, and underside. |
| High rear fullness | **Pass.** Rear volume stays high and broad instead of collapsing into a low bulge. |
| Visible lower-rear undercut | **Weak / fail.** There is a general bottom tuck, but not a clear lower-rear undercut. It resolves as the generic lower corner of the rounded volume. |
| Continuous three-quarter roll | **Partial.** The roll is smooth, but it is a global superellipsoid roll. It does not clearly carry a shallow face zone into a rear-full support, and the two mirrored three-quarter/profile presentations do not describe that transition consistently enough. |
| Broad pole-free closures | **Pass.** Crown, underside, and end closures are broad, with no visible pole point, dimple, or pinch. |
| Forbidden reads | **Fail.** Rounded box/mattress in front, rear, top, and bottom; egg-like in profile; long vertical wall at the face side. No obvious dimple or pinch. |

## Largest visible discrepancies

1. The front, rear, top, and bottom silhouettes are dominated by a rounded
   rectangle/superellipse. This is the clearest read and directly violates the
   no-rounded-box gate.
2. The side silhouette reads as a tall egg with an extended near-vertical face
   wall, rather than a shallow face plane attached to a rear-full stuffed
   cushion.
3. The face zone is not spatially legible as its own shallow support region;
   the whole front participates in one continuous inflated wall.
4. The lower-rear undercut is too weak and too evenly rounded to remain
   readable once a visible hair coupon is seated over it.
5. The three-quarter forms are smooth but generic: they round every transition
   at nearly the same frequency instead of preserving the reference-directed
   distinction between face plane, crown roll, rear fullness, and lower-rear
   tuck.

## Hair-coupon viability

Not viable at this gate. A visible coupon would either inherit the bulky
rounded-box/egg support at the temples and lower rear, or would need to hide
most of the receiver to avoid exposing the wrong macro form. The underlying
surface needs a genuinely shallow, limited face zone and a broader, higher
rear mass with a plainly readable but still broad lower-rear tuck.

## Required next macro change

Replace the globally rounded superellipsoid read with a rear-full stuffed
cushion profile: shorten and soften the long face-side wall into a shallow
local plane, keep the rear high, and make the lower-rear silhouette turn inward
earlier over a broad span without creating a dimple, pinch, or pole. Preserve
the current broad closures while making those regions remain distinct through
both three-quarter views.
