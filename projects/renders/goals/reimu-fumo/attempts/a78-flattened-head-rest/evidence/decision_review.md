# A78 decision review — choose a purpose-built flattened head rest

## Verdict

Proceed with one purpose-built face/head-rest module before attempting more
hair. Preserve every hair object as frozen context, replace only
`Head_Cushion_Manual_Target` in a disposable copy, and build the receiver from
explicit front and rear fabric panels joined by a gusset. Do not reuse the A75
salvage cushion, A77 loft, Subdivision-followed-by-Solidify mantle, or a sphere.

## Strongest objection

A75 already showed that a face-cushion-only change can be visually secondary
because frozen hair owns most of the head silhouette. Repeating that boundary
could produce another locally neat object without advancing the whole plush.

## Evidence and alternatives

| Alternative | Evidence for it | Decisive problem | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tune A77 coupled head/mantle/leaf | Exact sparse topology and most macro metrics passed | Frozen face fit failed; independent audit found canopy/card representation failures and weak contact gates | Reject |
| Build hair over the existing receiver | Directly attacks the helmet silhouette | The receiver itself is the rounded support that new hair would inherit; A77 showed the support/contact boundary is unresolved | Defer |
| Head-rest-only module | Isolates the failed interface and supplies a reusable support for later hair | A75 warns that visual effect can be masked | Proceed only with a new panel/gusset representation and an affected-region visual gate |
| Whole-model materials | Reversible and improves surface realism | A77 material audit found geometry is still the bottleneck | Reject now |
| Live sculpt | Natural route for soft form | A76 proved the available control surface lacks genuine pointer strokes | Unavailable |

## Why this is not A75 repeated

A75 rendered an inherited spherical salvage cushion and rejected its muzzle,
cavity ring, side bulge, and detached beige islands. A78 begins from those
failure categories and authors a shallow 2.5D sewn pillow: a broad near-planar
front field, a separate rear panel, and an explicit perimeter gusset. Its front
sampling density is chosen around frozen eyes, mouth, hair aperture, and cheek
roll rather than inherited from a sphere or a low-resolution closed loft.

## Stop rule

Return before a beauty packet if signed witness clearances, evaluated depth,
panel topology, or hair-root integration fail. After at most one named local
correction, reset if the exposed face still reads as a muzzle, sphere, square
card, cavity, or detached island, or if rung 003 remains preferred in the
affected front and three-quarter regions.
