# A78 result — head-rest boundary rejected before geometry

## Outcome

Reset before Blender mutation. The purpose-built head-rest proposal does not
own enough visible pixels to address the dominant failure and its proposed
front/rear/gusset construction repeats receiver representations already
rejected as mattress, box, egg, or muzzle. No candidate was built or rendered;
the protected rung and tracked reusable asset remain unchanged.

## Decisive evidence

- A72 and A73 already built mechanically clean, pole-free analytic receiver
  cages. A73 used `9 x 9` cap patches and a `32`-sample perimeter yet scored
  `4/10` and remained a rounded box/mattress/egg.
- A75 tested the exact receiver-only boundary in frozen-hair context. Rung 003
  was preferred in all three affected views; the replacement formed a muzzle,
  cavity ring, bulbous side exposure, and detached beige islands.
- Only about `28%` of the receiver is visible in front, `16%` in either
  three-quarter, `4%` in profile, and `0%` in rear. The rejected hair shell
  therefore remains the dominant pixel owner.
- A77's absolute review identified the deep, uniformly rounded brown helmet as
  the dominant failure and its terminal strategy preserved the seated face
  cushion while replacing the crown/rear hair field.
- Deterministic cloth pressure is not material novelty here. A56B already ran
  explicit front/rear cuts plus a welded gusset and converged reproducibly to
  the same uniform foam-mattress read; A14 and A42 add nonconvergence,
  overinflation, collapse, and no-effect counterexamples.

Criteria 001--008 remain unverified because the module returned at strategy
preflight and made no candidate claim.

## Process result and next strategy

The deeper process review worked: it compared the proposed representation to
the full attempt history before paying for another build/render cycle. A
syntax-only reusable validation scaffold and read-only measurements may be
retained, but they are not model progress and must not delay the next visible
candidate.

Reverse the boundary. Preserve `Head_Cushion_Manual_Target`, all seven seated
face witnesses, bow, body, clothing, cameras, lighting, and materials. Replace
the complete helmet-owning cap/rear field with explicitly authored paired hair
surfaces that provide crown, profile, and rear coverage at the first visual
gate. Do not copy a concentric receiver swath, use uniform Solidify as the form
generator, or render a bald/partial rear stage.
