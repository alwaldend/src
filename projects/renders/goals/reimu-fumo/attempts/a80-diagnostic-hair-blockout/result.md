# A80 result — diagnostic hair blockouts reset at the first pixel gate

## Decision

Reset both A80 representations. Neither is preferred to protected rung 003,
neither is a candidate, and neither may enter the tracked reusable model.

## Work and observable result

Two independent isolated authors tested complete coarse hair assemblies in the
unchanged whole-plush context. Each assembly had to provide a crown field,
rear mass, and asymmetric returning lock before its first render.

- The sculpted/copy-warp lane rendered front, profile, and rear in 19.855
  seconds. It exposes a massive beige rear/profile region and preserves the
  helmet/cuboid silhouette.
- The constructed lane rendered front, three-quarter, and rear in 13.981
  seconds. It exposes a large beige crown patch and reads as an arch band,
  rear balloon, and detached banana-shaped lock.

Both variants trigger the attempt's categorical baldness, helmet, detached
root, and wrong-silhouette vetoes. Material, seam, density, or promotion work
cannot repair those macro failures, so the lanes stopped immediately.

## Process result

The diagnostic-first gate order worked, but the complete loop still missed its
budget. A79 spent 94 minutes 30 seconds before its first whole-character
pixels; A80 reached them after 50 minutes 41.672 seconds, 43 minutes 48.328
seconds faster but still 38 minutes 41.672 seconds beyond the 12-minute target.
Only 33.901 seconds of two Blender cycles and 33.708 seconds of rendering were
required. The remaining latency came before invocation and in the review
handoff, including duplicated one-off runners. No promotion-grade validation
was performed on either dead representation. The next work unit must change
the coverage/interface strategy, reuse the diagnostic runner, act on the
two-view pair immediately, and keep unrelated repository coordination off the
first-pixel critical path.
