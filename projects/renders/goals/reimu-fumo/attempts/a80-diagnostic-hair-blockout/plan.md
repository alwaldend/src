## Bounded hypothesis

A visually coherent but coarse brown hair assembly can fix the macro failure
with three complete roles: a full soft crown cover behind the retained fringe,
a compact rounded rear mass that narrows beneath the bow and tucks at the nape,
and one integrated asymmetric side/rear lock. The blockout must read as one
manufactured plush hair assembly without reverting to a spherical helmet.

Two isolated blockout authors may test materially different surface layouts
from the same frozen rung. Each must include all three roles; partial or bald
probes are invalid. The variants are never merged.

## Diagnostic acceptance

- Brown hair continuously owns the whole crown and rear; zero obvious beige
  bald patch in front, controlling three-quarter/profile, or rear.
- Crown reads soft and compact, not spherical, cuboid, or concentric with the
  receiver.
- Rear is rounded and layered with an irregular tucked nape, not a vertical
  rectangle.
- The asymmetric lock curves out of the crown/rear assembly and returns toward
  it; it is neither a remote strip nor a planar paddle.
- Retained front fringe stays readable and the candidate is preferred to rung
  003 in both controlling views.
