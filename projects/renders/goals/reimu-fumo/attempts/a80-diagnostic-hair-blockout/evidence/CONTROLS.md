## Observable requirements

1. **Complete brown ownership.** Front, both 3Qs, both profiles, rear, and both
   rear arcs show continuous brown coverage over the crown and rear. There is
   no obvious beige/support bald patch, even where the bow parts or occludes
   the root.
2. **Front crown envelope.** After front alignment residual is at most
   `.015 Wh`, crown full widths at `v = .052/.133/.242/.351/.459/.568` read
   approximately `.408/.674/.837/.908/.948/.976 Wh` (`+/- .03 Wh`). The crown
   grows gradually from the bow root; it is not a constant-radius dome.
3. **Front layer order.** The new crown stays behind the retained fringe and
   cheek locks. The exposed beige aperture remains about `.603 x .603 Wh`;
   the original asymmetric blunt central fringe and both separate cheek locks
   remain legible. No duplicate brown rim, beige island, dark cavity, or gap
   wider than one review pixel appears at the hairline.
4. **Two profile contributions.** Profiles visibly contain a compact close
   crown/rear field plus an independent bowed free leaf. Total brown depth is
   `1.14 +/- .05 Wh` in frames `10/11` and `1.19--1.23 Wh` (uncertainty
   `.06 Wh`) in frames `25/26`; the unequal silhouettes survive direct review.
5. **Soft integrated leaf.** The asymmetric leaf emerges from a bow-hidden
   crown/rear root, bows away, tapers toward a free lower point, and visually
   returns toward the assembly. Its apparent edge is thin (`.015--.03 Wh`)
   and softly rolled; it never reads as a remote strip, rod, paddle, or board.
6. **Compact rounded rear.** At frame `18`, the combined brown rear is about
   `.94 +/- .04 Wh` wide and `1.16 +/- .05 Wh` crown-to-lowest-lobe, with its
   maximum width near `.70 +/- .04` of the height. It narrows beneath the bow
   and toward the nape instead of forming vertical sides.
7. **Irregular nape.** The lower rear edge has four or five broad unequal lobes
   with `.10--.14 Wh` notch-to-tip relief. The silhouette is tucked and softly
   stuffed, not straight, shelf-like, periodically scalloped, or made from
   identical separate paddles.
8. **Diagonal rear overlap.** Across frames `17--24`, a broad foreground leaf
   remains visibly asymmetric and shifts laterally as the turn proceeds. The
   registered target is roughly `.45--.50 Wh` visible width, `.98--1.05 Wh`
   root-to-tip drop, and `.15--.25 Wh` lateral drift. T-junctions must describe
   one continuous overlap, not detached islands or a symmetric center split.
9. **Manufactured plush read.** Every transition is softly padded and rooted;
   the crown, rear mass, and leaf read as one sewn fabric-hair assembly with
   subtle asymmetric stuffing. Smooth shading, fuzz, or copied brown material
   cannot substitute for the correct silhouette and layer order.

## Categorical vetoes

Reject immediately if any affected fixed view shows: a bald beige/support
region; helmet, egg, skull, sphere, rounded box, or taut shell; curtain, cape,
card, blade, full-width rear sheet, or rectangular hem; detached/floating root
or exposed root tab; straight or periodic nape; symmetric rear split or equal
paddles; lost diagonal overlap; a mirrored Profile B; a second hairline or
small round face opening; hard plastic/thick-panel read; or a camera/view scale
changed to hide a mismatch. One veto overrides all relative improvement.

## Better than rung 003

Do not call A80 better merely because it differs from rung 003. It is better
only if it first clears every categorical veto and then, in the same fixed
views, all of the following are plainly true:

- the front retains rung 003's complete brown coverage while replacing its
  smooth concentric dome with the canonical widening, asymmetric crown;
- both profiles replace the spherical cap silhouette with a compact field plus
  a distinct bowed leaf, without making either side a mirror of the other;
- the rear replaces rung 003's centered, near-symmetric petal mass with the
  canonical broad lower width, irregular nape, and moving diagonal overlap;
- all roots and transitions remain visually connected, and no retained fringe,
  lock, bow, face, or garment relationship regresses; and
- the candidate is preferred to rung 003 from the exact front **and** its
  weakest registered 3Q/profile, then survives the rear and opposite-side
  regression views.

If any item is ambiguous at 320--512 px, it has not earned promotion work.
