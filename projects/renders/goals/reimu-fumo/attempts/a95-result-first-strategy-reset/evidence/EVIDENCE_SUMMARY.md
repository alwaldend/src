## Direct visual comparison

Against the canonical front, rung003's largest whole-image defects are:

1. the head/body relationship is too tall and narrow instead of a broad head
   over a compact seated lower mass;
2. hair is a smooth hard helmet with shallow card-like locks rather than
   layered stuffed/felt construction; and
3. skirt, sleeves, and feet remain rigid/disconnected CG proxies rather than a
   compressed sewn seated assembly.

These are coupled representation defects. Adding detail, pile, stitches, or a
rig before the complete silhouette improves would preserve the wrong parent.

## Process finding

The early visual gate improved falsification speed: A91/A92 stopped weak full
subjects before refinement. It did not improve the missing modeling control.
A94 then spent most of its work on a provenance harness and pre-event state
assertions, and still crossed input with an uninspected black baseline. That is
evidence that the process temporarily optimized audit machinery over the model
and checked file existence/hash where it needed to inspect pixels.

The next strategy must therefore produce complete-subject pixels first and
make instrumentation subordinate to a result-bearing edit. It should start
from the visual high water unless there is concrete evidence that its visible
representation cannot accept the chosen correction.
