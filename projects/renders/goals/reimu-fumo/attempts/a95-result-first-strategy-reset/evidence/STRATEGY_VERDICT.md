- bodice, collars, and cravat;
- all retained `Sleeve44P` padded panels, roots, pleats, and stitches;
- cameras, lights, materials, and scene render state.

The editable owner is only rung003's existing lower stack: seat pad, red skirt

`out/reimu_fumo_attempt_053_rung3_file_audit/FILE_AUDIT.md`.

### Operation

Use sparse Edit-mode/BMesh/proportional coordinate edits recorded as per-object
shape-key deltas. Pin upper attachment rows and carry stitch/ruffle curves with
their owning surfaces. Retain separate object identities, topology, vertex
counts, modifier order, and materials. Do not add a generator, global affine,
new visible owner, remesh, join, topology change, material, rig, or detail.

The single coupled objective is to replace the tall narrow lower silhouette
with a compact pooled seated rest: broaden side footprint toward the canonical
`1.15–1.20 Wh` band, reduce the current excessive whole-character
height/width relationship, pool the skirt rather than forming a cone, and tuck
two stuffed foot pods partly under the hem with broad plausible ground contact.

### First-pixel and keep gate

Render front, exact side, and worst three-quarter immediately after the first
coherent state. Absolute review precedes a blinded A/B against exact rung003.
Keep only if every view:

- clearly reads as a compact pooled seated skirt rather than cone, tent, ramp,
  cape, or floor rail;
- shows two partly occluded stuffed foot pods and broad believable contact;
- preserves Reimu recognition and all frozen interfaces;
- introduces no sleeve/collar gap, clipping, or floating; and
- reaches at least internal `6/10` for lower silhouette, construction, and
  contact while being clearly preferred to rung003.

Numeric measurements may veto a regression but cannot qualify weak pixels.
At most one correction is allowed only after the category already passes and
exactly one localized contact defect remains. A tie, invisible change,
categorical recurrence, or protected-interface regression resets the attempt.

### Animation readiness

Rung003 has no animation system to preserve. Future readiness is protected by
keeping separate panels, feet, and leg roots; stable object identities and
topology; pinned attachment loops; and reversible shape-key deltas. Rig only a
visual survivor.
