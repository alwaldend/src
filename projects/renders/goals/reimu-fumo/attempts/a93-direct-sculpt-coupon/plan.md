## Hypothesis

A sparse, direct Edit-mode proportional-deformation path can produce broad,
predictable, independently directed low-frequency form changes without the
tiny support, dimples, or camera-only effects that invalidated prior scripted
sculpt strokes. If it cannot, the current tool surface does not support the
manual-style blockout required by the recovery plan.

## Work unit

1. Create one disposable, connected, low-resolution rounded cushion coupon in
   `out/reimu_fumo_attempt_093_direct_sculpt_coupon/author/` using pinned
   Blender 5.2.1. This is an interface specimen, not a character candidate.
2. Freeze a baseline `.blend`, mesh-coordinate digest, topology inventory,
   cameras, lights, and front/right/three-quarter renders.
3. Apply exactly three sequential sparse proportional edits in non-collinear
   dominant directions: broad side expansion (`+X`), broad depth shaping
   (`+Y`), and broad lower support lift/compression (`+Z`). Selected seeds,
   falloff support, and displacement vectors must be explicit and auditable.
4. After each edit, freeze a snapshot and the same three views. Record
   per-vertex displacement counts, connected support, support bounds, signed
   direction ratios, topology, object transforms, camera digests, and pixel
   differences against the immediately preceding state.
5. Build one inspectable comparison sheet and run one independent correction
   review on the exact frozen pixels before interpreting the authoring method.

## Pass gate

All three edits must satisfy the discriminator in `DECISION_REVIEW.md`. The
final coupon must visibly preserve three distinct broad deformations, read as
a coherent soft form, and show no dimple, hard ring, disconnected support,
global object transform, topology change, or altered camera. Technical
operator success alone is insufficient.

Passing authorizes exactly one complete low-resolution manual-style Reimu
Fumo sculpt from a fresh isolated file. It does not pass any final goal
criterion. Failure resets A93 and closes this authoring modality.

## Boundaries and stop rules

- Do not mutate the tracked Blend, rung003, any reference, or prior attempt.
- Do not use native scripted sculpt strokes, analytic whole-form fields,
  panels, lofts, primitive stacks, detailed costume work, materials, or rigging.
- Do not repair a failed deformation by changing strength, support, camera, or
  topology inside this attempt.
- Stop at the first evidence that an edit is local, visually meaningless, or
  merely a transformed camera/object.
- Keep all scratch, caches, renders, logs, and snapshots under this attempt's
  ignored `out/` directory.

## Ready workstreams

- One author owns the isolated coupon and exact snapshots.
- The coordinator owns durable goal state, invariant/hash verification, and
  comparison assembly.
- Exactly one fresh reviewer will judge the frozen comparison packet after the
  author finishes. No concurrent mutation of a shared Blend is allowed.
