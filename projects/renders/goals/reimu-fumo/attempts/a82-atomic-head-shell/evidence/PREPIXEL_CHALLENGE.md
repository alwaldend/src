authoring one coarse union removes A81's competition between a beige receiver
and incomplete brown coverage. Direct broad edits can redistribute volume
without first committing to a receiver equation, cap, card set, or visual

- rounded-square/superellipse outline, broad flat top with near-vertical

### Weak three-quarter

- one uninterrupted convex arc from crown through rear to nape;
- deep egg, balloon, rounded box, mattress, canopy, or vertical rear wall;
- a high-centered rear bulge with no lower-rear weight, turn-in, or undercut;
- a box corner, planar slab, hard vertical face/rear break, or faceted block;
- absent broad return silhouette, or a return expressed as a narrow horn,
  tube, banana, fin, paddle, or remote strip;
- front improvement purchased by worse depth, rear bulk, scale, or bow/body
  relation; or
- no clear macro preference over rung 003 in this same view.
