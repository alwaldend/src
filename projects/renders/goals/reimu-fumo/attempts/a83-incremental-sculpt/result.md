## Work and observable result

A83 tested isolated reversible edits and replacement coupons around the C1b
appearance checkpoint. Hair cycles C13--C16 failed to achieve both compact
coverage and layered fabric construction. C18's separated liner, mantle, and
three rear leaves retained a helmet-like mantle and produced disconnected,
inflated pods. Sleeve cycles C17 and C18 changed the local representation but
read respectively as a rigid paddle and an inflated mitten. All were undone.
Stage R2 remains useful isolated deformation scaffolding, but it does not
validate appearance or animation readiness.

The C19 whole-model audit found that the remaining failures are coupled under
two structural owners rather than separable surface defects:

1. the cranial attachment and visible-envelope scaffold joins the deep
   helmet, layered-hair, and crown-seated-bow failures; and
2. the seated torso/pelvis and garment-contact scaffold joins the standing
   dress cone, tube-like sleeves, pooled-hem, foot-occlusion, and ground-contact
   failures.

These owners meet at the collar and shoulder interface and move most visible
macro extrema. C1b is therefore categorically nonviable as a whole-model
direct-sculpt baseline. Continuing to accumulate local corrections on it
would preserve its name while replacing its structure and would repeat the
same failed representation.

Criterion 001 fails reference likeness. Criterion 003 fails plush
construction. Criterion 004 fails because implementation-blind reviews did
not reach the required scores and reported major visible defects. Criterion
006 remains incomplete and therefore unverified for an acceptable asset.
Criteria 002, 005, 007, and 008 remain unverified against a final accepted
candidate. No attempt is accepted.

## Process audit

A83 improved feedback discipline: isolated immutable copies, fixed views,
implementation-blind vetoes, and early stops prevented rejected coupons from
reaching the tracked asset. C18 also showed that rendering the same live
snapshot twice wastes the critical path; future cycles should save once and
send that immutable snapshot directly to the pinned batch renderer.

The process nevertheless spent too many cycles varying generated hair and
sleeve abstractions after the coupled macro scaffold had become the dominant
failure. The next attempt changes both the baseline and authoring modality:
start an empty task-owned scene, keep C1b hidden as a comparator, and directly
sculpt one coarse transformable owner at a time. The first bounded owner is a
shallow gusseted head cushion. It must pass registered multi-view silhouette
and construction gates before hair, bow, body, detail, materials, or rig work
begins.

## Protected state and next action

- Keep C1b byte-exact and hidden from the new candidate scene.

- Start A84 as a new coarse modular neutral sculpt.
