# A83 incremental sculpt plan

## Hypothesis

Rung 003 is a materially better base than every full-form replacement tried in
A80-A82. Its likeness can improve through isolated, reversible deformations of
the existing hair meshes while the beige face, bow, eyes, body, topology, and
materials remain intact.

## Cycle 1: upper-cap width

1. Work only on an isolated exact copy of rung 003.
2. Apply one soft inward-X deformation to the upper lateral region of
   `A44 continuous hair cap with smooth opening`; do not move Y or Z.
3. Save an immutable candidate and render only exact front and three-quarter.
4. Keep the edit only if the pair is implementation-blind preferred to rung
   003, the upper dome is visibly less helmet-like, and no face opening, bow
   seat, fringe root, lock contact, or eye visibility regresses. Otherwise
   restore the isolated parent without repair.

## Later cycles

Only after cycle 1 is accepted, reshape the five existing fringe panels as a
separate one-change cycle. Do not replace the whole head or generate a new
head/hair representation.

## Timing

Freeze each deformation quickly and render immediately. Mechanical and
promotion checks remain deferred until a pixel-surviving result exists.
