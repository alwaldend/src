## Whole-process evaluation

The useful part of A77 was not the model; it was shortening the rejection
loop. A pure topology preflight found the disconnected mantle before Blender,
the deterministic build exposed the interface conflict before rendering, and
independent review prevented numerical gate-passing from being mistaken for
visual quality. The waste came from an over-delegated backup branch whose
recursive audits duplicated evidence instead of publishing a candidate; it
was interrupted once the primary build became decisive.

The next work unit must be smaller in geometry ownership but judged in the
whole plush. Preserve the existing face support and visible identity witnesses.
Replace only the crown/rear field that owns the helmet silhouette, with
explicit paired panel surfaces and a bounded thickness/contact proof. This
avoids both the A75 face-only dead end and A77's three-form coupling.
