# Reimu Fumo process audit after A74--A76

## Verdict

The goal protocol is producing trustworthy rejection evidence, but it is not
currently producing the requested result. The canonical goal is open and
active at resource version 19, with no active attempt, no accepted attempt,
and no accepted result digest. A68--A76 are nine consecutive resets. The best
whole-plush artifact is still protected rung 003.

A74--A76 closed in about 42 minutes total, so Blender startup, rendering,
hashing, and record checkpointing are not the main latency. In that interval
the process produced two visual candidates and one unmodified interface probe,
but no candidate was preferred to rung 003. It also retained 2,909 lines of
plan/result/evidence prose across the three attempts. Much of that prose is
good, but the ratio of analysis to improved pixels is now wrong.

The next critical-path attempt should **not** be a material pass. A fresh
implementation-blind review of rung 003 finds the dominant defect is the deep,
uniformly rounded head/hair helmet; material cannot create the missing shallow
base, profile undercut, layer breaks, or irregular rear silhouette. Applying
fiber to the current model would make a fuzzy helmet. A reusable shader coupon
is worthwhile only as parallel, non-blocking work and must not count as a
fidelity cycle.

Proceed with a sparse, directly editable, whole-context head/hair macro module.
It must reach pixels before a detailed topology contract is written. If that
module passes, construct the visible fabric-panel assembly on the passed rest
form. Keep a shader coupon parallel and optional.

## Reusable successes

Retain these; they reduce uncertainty or protect quality.

1. **Rung 003 as whole-context baseline.** It is not approval quality, but its
   bow, expression, outfit, complete rear coverage, body, cameras, and lights
   make it the least misleading comparison host.
2. **Immutable-copy discipline.** Protected hashes, single-save candidates,
   clean reopen under pinned Blender 5.2.1, and geometry/visibility
   fingerprints reliably prevent accidental regression.
3. **Fast fixed-view packets.** Front, both three-quarters, profile, and rear
   renders expose representation failures quickly. Rendering is not the
   bottleneck.
4. **Implementation-blind review.** Reviewers repeatedly distinguish operator
   or topology success from visual success. Keep this gate, but reserve it for
   candidates that first survive a cheap coordinator veto.
5. **Reference assets and measured observations.** Reuse A74's source curves
   and scanlines as projection controls, A75's face-aperture/cheek-chin contract
   as a later visible-field control, and all canonical-turn frames for depth
   and layer order. Do not reuse their speculative hidden geometry.
6. **A52 whole-subject hull as a veto only.** It can reject gross extent but
   cannot supply component surfaces, seams, or hair construction.
7. **A28 rear felt-panel V4 as a successful isolated contour witness.** Its
   `.977984` rear IoU and blind likeness `8.8/10` show that direct visible
   contour ownership can work. Reuse the contour/layer lesson; do not assume
   its old root interface can be transplanted unchanged.
8. **A76 negative interface finding.** Do not schedule another synthetic
   `bpy.ops.sculpt.brush_stroke` attempt unless a genuine pointer-event path
   becomes available.

Do not reuse A74 geometry, A75 C0/A66 salvage geometry, the A76 probe as an
artistic parent, analytic receiver families, or independently extruded hair
cards.

## Stable defect ledger

The same failures have been renamed often enough to hide recurrence. Use the
following stable IDs in subsequent plans and reviews.

| Stable ID | Definition | Repeated witnesses | Current count interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `D-HEAD-HELMET` | One deep, smooth mass owns crown, profile, and rear; reads as helmet/egg/mattress/box rather than shallow support plus draping layers | A57, A58, A66, A69, A72, A73, rung 003; A74 became a canopy variant | Representation family closed; no more closed analytic shells or receiver-first claims |
| `D-PANEL-CARD` | Visible fabric components read as flat masks, blades, fins, paddles, or rigid cards with floating roots | A34, A46, A55, A58, A62--A65, A70, A74 | Independent extrusion family closed until a passed rest form exists |
| `D-FACE-MASK` | Beige carrier reads as plaque/card/ball/muzzle with a rim, cavity, or side-on oval | A57, A66, A74, A75 | Defer isolated receiver work; later consume the A75 broad-plateau contract in a passed hair interface |
| `D-REAR-ROOT` | Bald rear, symmetric curtain/cape, floating root, or lost attachment/contact | A69, A70, A74 and earlier panel attempts | Every head coupon must include complete rear coverage and root continuity at its first pixel gate |
| `D-SCULPT-SUPPORT` | Synthetic brush reports success but moves too few useful vertices or produces dimples | A11, A68, A71; A76 reconfirmed capability absence | Closed until real pointer events or a materially different authoring control exists |
| `D-HIDDEN-OPT` | Detailed work optimizes an under-observed receiver or interface while the visible silhouette owner remains frozen | A72, A73, A75 | Stop before implementation; work on the visible pixel owner instead |

Any recurrence of one of these IDs is a reset, not a reason for another
parameter correction. A candidate may use related concepts only when its
representation is materially different and the plan says how the first render
will discriminate it.

## Avoidable feedback-loop waste

### 1. The first pixel arrives after excessive representation commitment

A74 accumulated a 392-line design review and a 393-line topology algorithm
before producing a front image whose square face card, peach canopy, and blade
locks are immediately disqualifying. Source provenance and topology were
excellent; they did not protect against the visible category error.

Correction: before documenting production topology, make one crude but
whole-context front/profile/rear blockout. A plan should name the pixel change,
not fully specify a mesh that has not survived a thumbnail.

### 2. Some attempt interfaces guaranteed their own failures

A74 hid all rear hair owners while deferring rear replacement to P1, yet
judged P0 in rear whole-model context. The bald rear was guaranteed. A75 then
froze the dominant rejected hair shell and tested a secondary receiver whose
visible share is only about 28% front, 16% three-quarter, 4% profile, and 0%
rear. Both decompositions were mechanically tidy but visually low leverage.

Correction: a module must include every object needed to avoid a guaranteed
categorical veto in its required views. “Small” means small uncertainty and a
clean interface, not an arbitrarily low object count.

### 3. Known-rejected parents are being used as cheap candidates

A75 produced a valuable new face contract, but its only visual candidate was
the exact A66 salvage cushion, whose family had already been retired. The
result reconfirmed the known face-ball/mask failure. Cheap repetition is still
opportunity cost when it cannot change the next decision.

Correction: before build, search the stable defect ledger by input hash and
representation. A retired parent may be used only as a control, never as the
new hypothesis.

### 4. Strategy review sometimes occurs after the attempt starts

A76's adversarial review found that A68 and A71 had already run the supposedly
new foreground/live `VIEW_3D` route. The correct reset took under six minutes,
but it did not need a canonical attempt or saved probe.

Correction: capability checks and “is this materially new?” review happen in
the unfrozen preflight. Start a goal attempt only when the next artifact can
discriminate a live hypothesis. A rejected preflight is a short process note,
not an attempt.

### 5. Evidence repeats rather than compounds

A74--A76 contain 2,909 lines across plans, results, and evidence. Exact hashes,
source roles, old failure descriptions, and acceptance prose recur in several
files. This helps auditability but burdens the coordinator and hides the one
decision that matters.

Correction: link immutable prior evidence by path/digest. Per attempt retain:

- one plan of at most roughly one screen/page;
- the executable parameter/control file;
- one render manifest and comparison sheet;
- one concise blind verdict if the candidate survives self-veto; and
- one result/process delta containing only what changed in knowledge.

Long topology specifications are earned only after a visual blockout passes.

### 6. Rejection discipline lacks a monotonic working ladder

The process prevents bad promotion but treats every below-final candidate as a
reset. That is safe, yet nine resets leave no intermediate improvement.

Correction: keep final acceptance at 8/10, but freeze a new **working rung**
whenever a bounded module is clearly preferred to its parent in every affected
view, passes whole-model regressions, and introduces no categorical veto. A
working rung is not the tracked deliverable and does not weaken final criteria;
it gives the next module a genuinely better parent.

## Next bounded DAG: three modules maximum

```text
M1 head/hair macro assembly  -------->  M3 constructed head integration
                                          ^
M2 fabric shader coupon (parallel) -------|
```

M1 is the critical path. M2 may run concurrently but may not delay M1 or be
counted as model-fidelity progress. M3 starts only if M1 passes.

### M1 — sparse whole-context head/hair macro assembly

**Question:** Can a directly edited sparse control assembly replace the deep
helmet with a shallow stuffed base plus separately readable crown/fringe and
rear reach, without cards or a bald rear?

**Boundary:** isolated copy of protected rung 003; preserve bow, eyes, body,
clothing, feet, cameras, and lights. Replace the complete silhouette-owning
head/hair boundary together. Use only a small, auditable control set and
explicit BMesh/Edit-mode transforms. Do not use a superellipsoid, per-height
loft, visual-hull surface, synthetic sculpt stroke, old helmet deformation, or
independently extruded detail cards.

The first blockout needs only four readable roles: shallow face/head support,
compact crown/front field, one broad asymmetric rear/background mass, and one
independent free rear leaf. Cheek locks may be blunt proxies only if needed to
close the front silhouette. It must have complete rear coverage at gate zero.

**First artifact:** within one build loop, render 320 px front, worse profile,
rear, and worse three-quarter beside rung 003 and the controlling source
crops. Add semantic-ID silhouettes; no seams, fibers, detailed topology,
rigging, or material work.

**Pass:** clearly preferred to rung 003 in all four affected views; no
`D-HEAD-HELMET`, `D-PANEL-CARD`, `D-REAR-ROOT`, sphere, anatomical head, or
face-card read; macro identity and silhouette at least 6/10; profile/rear
extrema within existing source uncertainty. Freeze the exact candidate as a
new working rung.

**Stop:** coordinator self-veto on the first categorical defect. Permit one
bounded control-point correction only if every view improves. Do not request
blind review for an obvious fail.

### M2 — reusable fabric shader coupon, parallel and non-blocking

**Question:** Can one procedural, physically scaled material set improve the
intended-medium read without geometry tricks, excessive noise, or external
assets?

Build neutral flat, convex, folded-edge, and compressed-contact swatches for
brown short pile, beige fleece, red/white woven felt or cotton, yellow cloth,
and black velour. Use pinned Blender 5.2.1, procedural nodes, and controlled
close-up lighting. Record exact node parameters and physical fiber/bump scale.

**Pass:** independent close-up review prefers each coupon to the current clay
material, with no plastic, sandpaper, glitter, procedural-cloud, or silhouette
fuzz artifact. Preserve the material library for M3 or later modules.

**Stop:** do not apply it across rung 003 as an A77 fidelity candidate. If it
only makes smooth geometry fuzzy, retain or reject the coupon without claiming
model progress.

### M3 — constructed head integration on the passed M1 rest form

**Depends on:** M1 pass. M2 is optional input if its coupons pass.

Retopologize the visible crown/fringe field, broad cheek locks, rear
background mass, and free rear leaf as finite-thickness stuffed fabric panels
seated on the passed macro rest form. Use A74 source curves for visible
boundaries, A75's broad face-plateau/cheek-chin contract for the exposed beige
field, and A28's successful rear contour only as a witness. Do not preserve
legacy root coordinates when they conflict with the passed surface.

Render the full fixed five-view A/B packet plus material-ID masks. Require
whole-head identity, construction, and contact each at least 6/10, direct
preference over M1 and rung 003, zero bald rear or support leakage, and no
return of any stable defect. If it passes, freeze it as the next working rung;
only then schedule detailed seams, bow/body contact, materials, and rigging.

## Operating rules for the next loop

1. Run the stable-defect and material-novelty preflight before opening a goal
   attempt. A repeated family closes outside the attempt.
2. Name three or fewer expected pixel changes and their controlling views.
   Every modeled control must serve one of them.
3. Reach a whole-context thumbnail before writing a production topology
   specification.
4. Self-veto obvious failures; use independent blind review only for survivors.
5. One candidate plus at most one bounded correction per module.
6. Reuse existing references and contracts by digest; do not restate them.
7. Freeze any honestly better working rung even when it is below final 8/10;
   never promote it to the tracked asset until the full goal criteria pass.
8. Keep one Blender writer. Parallelize reference packet assembly, shader
   coupons, masks/overlays, and blind review against immutable inputs.
9. Report progress to the user at each visible artifact or decisive preflight,
   not only after a complete attempt closes.

## Bottom line

The protocol itself is not the fundamental blocker. Its isolation, fixed
views, and honest review are valuable. Its present application is too eager to
formalize speculative representations and too willing to count falsification
as sufficient momentum. The next loop must optimize for an improved working
rung: pixels first, concise evidence, stable defect IDs, and a critical path
owned by the coupled head/hair macro form.
