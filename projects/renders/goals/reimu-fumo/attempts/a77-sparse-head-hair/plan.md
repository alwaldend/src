## Hypothesis

A sparse, directly controlled, untextured head/hair macro assembly can express
the observed broad shallow head, low off-center fringe, side locks, and
separate rear layer more reliably than the rejected dense sculpt strokes,
formula fields, receiver lofts, or extruded card stack. Judging it immediately
in the complete plush prevents isolated-topology success from replacing visual
fidelity.

## Bounded work

1. Preserve all supplied references and use the canonical front plus all 30
   canonical-turn frames, with physical stills controlling fabric construction.
2. Inventory and fingerprint the parent. Hide rather than delete only the
   legacy objects whose pixels the new module replaces.
3. Build one low-density direct-control head/hair assembly and only the minimum
   face carrier/interface needed to see it. Preserve bow, lower body, sleeves,
   feet, cameras, and lighting as unchanged whole-model witnesses.
4. Produce cheap fixed front, profile, rear, and both three-quarter renders.
   Compare them to the exact rung-003 views and controlling reference views.
5. Inspect pixels implementation-blind. If the first packet clears the early
   gate, permit one bounded form correction and then run the full five-view
   regression. Do not add materials, fibers, stitches, rigging, or unrelated
   lower-body changes.

## Early stop and review gates

Stop after the first packet if any required view reads as a helmet, sphere,
egg, mattress, face card, bald shell, or detached panel stack; if face or eye
attachments float or clip; if the front silhouette misses a critical landmark
by more than `0.05 Wh`; or if an implementation-blind reviewer scores macro
silhouette below `6/10` or construction below `5/10`. A failed packet closes
the attempt; it is not polished into a second representation.

Affected criteria are 001--004. Criteria 005--008 remain unverified for this
disposable module. Fixed regressions are both three-quarter views, rear hair
coverage, face-applique seating, bow/head contact, and protected-input hashes.

## Parallel work and process limit

The coordinator is the sole goal writer. Four isolated read-only workstreams
run concurrently: blind pixel review, material feasibility, sparse topology
design, and end-to-end process audit. Their outputs can alter the first build
decision without racing the candidate. Material work is non-blocking and may
produce a later reusable coupon, but cannot advance the fidelity attempt while
macro geometry fails. The plan and result stay concise; rendered pixels, not
record volume, control the decision.
