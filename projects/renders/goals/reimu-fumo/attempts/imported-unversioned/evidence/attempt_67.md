## 67H — open crown yoke and root-mapped rear pockets

The head lane replaces A66H's closed fringe-to-rear mantle with a shared-
vertex shallow face cushion, an open front/crown/temple yoke terminating high
on a rear arch, and four broad S-course rear pockets. Candidate SHA-256 is
`bd6fb4a53b28d6b7d7c7277190bede375e3e1324fb071b82438c4b0d53a32f83`.

The topology differs on paper, but its evaluated pixels repeat the closed
family's decisive complaint. The front exposes a very large bald beige crown
and angular hair cut; side and obliques remain a deep faceted helmet/egg with
floating wire-like cards; rear becomes one cuboid backing rather than broad
overlapping leaves. Seven of eight registered front width rows fail, the crown
contains `2,751` exposed beige pixels, and rear H/W is `1.092` versus the
`1.15–1.35` gate. Both implementation-blind reviews score only `2–3/10` and
agree that one bounded correction cannot pass.

## 67L — direction-changing seated construction

The lower lane rejected its first four-long-panel draft before Blender because
it preserved the same waist-to-ground tension paths as the cape/ramp family.
Its revised packet uses a closed T-yoke torso/seat receiver, an independent
front apron, a U-shaped rear/side drop yoke, and a separate low horseshoe pool
facing. A real low transverse seam breaks every single-object rear-waist-to-
rear-floor path. Candidate SHA-256 is
`919dfee8fe28fb7e9ac5b049e18e52cd41786f7fa5fa29ff4005cc38d6f3ee0f`.

Every written mechanical gate passes, including the real direction-change
seam, broad contact, receiver/collar gap, registered widths, grain-path
descent, frozen context, and zero new foot crossings. The fixed pixels still
repeat the terminal visual failure: exact side is one tall near-straight cape/
ramp/tent from shoulder to hem; front and rear are rigid expanding trapezoids;
and the low horseshoe pool is swallowed rather than reading as a separate soft
return. The direction-change topology does not control the visible form.
