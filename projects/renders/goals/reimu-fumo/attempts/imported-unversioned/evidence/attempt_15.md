## Attempt 15 — direct native clay from isotropic tool stock

**Candidate:** planned
`out/reimu_fumo_attempt_015_icosphere_clay/checkpoint_family_00.blend`, SHA-256
pending, post-eleven-Grab neutral macro-sculpt stage, review packet
`attempt-015-native-icosphere-clay-reference-and-fixed-views`.

**Failure targeted:** every untouched generated solid preserved its generator,
the sharp cube preserved six planes, and the cloth simulation diverged before
review. Treating those setup forms as visual candidates repeatedly consumed
iterations without performing the sculpt-first workflow the user requested.

**Hypothesis:** A small isotropic sphere is topology-neutral clay rather than a
head generator: it has no front/rear axis, cap/rim boundary, plane, corner,
gusset, or final proportion. Broad verified native Grab strokes can build
crown, shoulder, lower cheek, underside, and depth outward from undersized
stock. A mandatory mid-family voxel remesh destroys the sphere topology after
the front macroform exists, and six smaller side strokes then establish lower
front and higher rear stuffing without another plate, cleft, or simulation.

**Structural difference from prior failures:** This is not the old procedural
ellipsoid: the stock is isotropic, `30–38%` under target, never rendered,
never nonuniformly scaled, and its topology is destroyed only after five
verified native strokes establish the front macroform. It is not the sharp
cube: no six planes or corners survive, and positive outward pulls build the
silhouette instead of subtracting corners until view edits meet as a cleft. It
is not another generator or physics result: no profile formula, cap/rim,
bevel, gusset, cloth solver, or final modifier determines the reviewed surface.

**Result:** Terminal preflight rejection. The dormant 1,353-line driver
compiled, but no candidate geometry or render was executed. Its SHA-256 was
`b8f53ef5ac8344aa3e605d503d1a14f5e8d252b0af2ef77f6049775a67fdfa6f`;
the complete temporary preflight record SHA-256 was
`348f39dc0d4961b5d716897452c8698cdf08f25b9f33fd1786a0d80d1ed24f68`.

The first planned stroke begins at `(0.205, 0.285)`, whose radial distance is
`0.3510697936 Wh`. That point is outside the untouched convex radius-`0.350`
icosphere, so its mandatory surface raycast cannot hit. Static and live
read-only Blender checks also proved that the inherited calibration would
execute every declared `0.27–0.42 P` radius as the same `300 px` brush and
both `0.24 P` radii as `150 px`; the record would nevertheless label them as
the requested fractions. The implementation discarded exact projection,
raycast, and sample evidence, could false-pass its symmetry probe, and did not
gate stroke direction or locality.

The review path was invalid independently of sculpt execution. It hashed the
current metrics and probes and checked them against those same current hashes,
did not restore a dirty live checkpoint when its filepath already matched,
omitted the planned side chord/roof/parallel-run gates, and lost terminal
evidence on exceptions. Its candidate-only fast images had no aligned
reference comparison. Its later montage independently resized full-doll
references beside a bare head, and the connected Blender Flatpak reported
`shutil.which("magick") == None`, so the montage command could not run there.

**Criterion results:** No visual, construction, reuse, or animation criterion
passes. Compile and Blender API-property checks pass only as preflight checks.
The exact protected tracked model remains unchanged.

**Decision:** Reject Attempt 15 without running its impossible first stroke.
Preserve its temporary driver and audit as negative evidence. Do not repair or
tune that frozen family in place.

### Progress and approach audit after attempt 15

- **Improved:** the updated skill guidance stopped an impossible family before
  it dirtied Blender, and its implementation-blind comparison requirement
  exposed review packets that looked rigorous but could not measure likeness.
- **Regressed or unchanged:** no pixels improved, and the planned family still
  treated a large set of scripted strokes as one delayed-feedback operation.
- **Absolute result:** there is no candidate. This is a workflow correction,
  not artistic progress.
- **Repeated-failure diagnosis:** the previous bare-head gates conflated the
  outer brown hair/head envelope (`1.03 Wh` tall) with the beige cushion hidden
  underneath it. The construction notes instead place that cushion near
  `0.84–0.90 Wh` tall. Optimizing bare clay toward `0.98–1.04 Wh` encouraged
  the tall, fat egg silhouette repeatedly rejected in the supplied references.
- **Evidence against continuing:** Attempt 15 is impossible as written; its
  discrete brush tiers are not its declared radii; and the unaligned packet
  cannot distinguish improved likeness from a better-lit generic object.
- **Highest-leverage unresolved problem:** sculpt the correctly owned beige
  cushion volume, with a broad gently convex front/rear and compact height,
  before the hair supplies the larger outer crown and rear envelope.
- **Approach decision:** retain native Sculpt Mode, but discard isotropic-stock
  dogma, fixed off-surface starts, delayed eleven-stroke review, mutable
  evidence, and full-doll-to-bare-head montage. Attempt 16 uses a disposable
  under-scale clay blank, exact live-projection brush sizes, small checkpointed
  families, and a frozen head-stage silhouette guide derived before execution.
