# A85 plan — constructed low-resolution head cage

## Hypothesis

A low-resolution quad cage whose topology explicitly owns the crown, cheek,
chin, front plane, rear plane, and side gusset can establish the measured
head-cushion silhouette without relying on localized brush pulls. Broad direct
sculpting can then add restrained stuffing softness without turning the form
into a box, sphere, egg, or helmet.

## Immutable inputs and protected state

- Canonical front and canonical 180-degree turn remain the controlling
  references; all supplied physical references remain construction vetoes.
- C1b remains a hidden immutable comparator only. Do not append or sculpt its
  visible-form meshes.
- A84 S02 remains process evidence, not the new base mesh.
- Keep the 25 cm whole-plush scale contract, fixed review cameras, and
  separated asset/review collections.

## One bounded owner

Create only `A85_HeadCushionCage` in a new task-owned scene. In Edit Mode,
construct a sparse, symmetric quad cage with explicit horizontal rows for the
crown shoulder, upper cheek, broad cheek, lower cheek, and chin. Use paired
front/rear planes joined by a shallow side gusset. Set the coarse front
silhouette to the measured `1.00 Wh` width, `0.94 Wh` height, `0.82 Wh` lower
width, crown rise at most `0.10 Wh`, and depth inside `0.55–0.68 Wh`.

Subdivision may round transitions but must preserve readable front and rear
planes. Do not add hair, bow, face graphics, body, seams, fibers, materials,
or rig controls. After the cage passes front/profile silhouettes, use at most
one broad direct-sculpt batch for slight stuffing asymmetry and flattening.

## Review loop and stop gate

Freeze every candidate as an immutable snapshot and clean-reopen it with the
repository-pinned Blender. First render a front/profile controlling pair; a
passing pair earns rear, both profiles, and both three-quarter views from the
same state.

Keep only if critical head landmarks are within `3% Wh`, depth extrema within
the frozen band, the central face remains broadly planar, and every view reads
as a shallow stuffed cushion. Reject immediately on a rounded box, sphere,
egg, helmet, human jaw, pinched row, twisted gusset, or hidden rear invention.
If two cage variants fail, change the row layout rather than adding sculpt
strokes or detail.

Scripts may prepare cameras, references, manifests, and renders, but must not
generate the artistic surface. Canonical artistic mutation remains with one
coordinator; immutable image review and measurement checks may run in
parallel.
