# A85 result — refine with a provisional head receiver

A85 closes as **refine**, not accept. It replaced the nonviable rounded-cube
workflow with a directly authored sparse quad cage and produced the immutable
S12 head receiver at SHA-256
`982da6404ea6edcbb4432903e67dad4ee5c130a203a5a5727a374b773fc9ad8a`.
No tracked reusable asset or Sisyphus scene was modified.

S12 passes every measurable bare-head foundation silhouette gate: height
`.976 Wh`, maximum depth `.628 Wh`, lower width `.825 Wh`, the complete cheek
band `.969-1.000 Wh`, crown shoulder `.738 Wh`, and crown rise `.094 Wh`.
The pinned six-view packet also passes projection regression: mirrored profile
and three-quarter pairs have matching centers, widths, and effectively equal
unshadowed silhouettes. Independent pixel review keeps S12 narrowly as a
provisional receiver without an egg, human jaw, or material geometric
asymmetry.

S12 is not standalone sculpt approval. Its exposed underside still shades as
a recessed fold/cavity. Multiple amplitude, curvature-distribution, depth,
and support-ring variants either preserved that cue or damaged passing
silhouettes, so further isolated head tuning was stopped. The next attempt
must construct a collar/body context that fully covers this hidden interface
with broad soft contact, no gap, no crossing, and no movement of the frozen
head envelope. If integration exposes the underside, reopen only that
interface rather than rebuilding the passing head silhouette.

All full-model criteria remain unverified: the rebuilt hair, face, bow, body,
seated pose, materials, rig, animation tests, and tracked delivery do not yet
exist.
