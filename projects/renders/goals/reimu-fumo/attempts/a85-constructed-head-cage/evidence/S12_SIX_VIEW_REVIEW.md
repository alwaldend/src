# A85 S12 six-view implementation-blind review

## Review isolation

I inspected only the exact pinned S12 six-view render/contact sheet and the
controlling reference images. I did not inspect the Blend file, topology,
scripts, object names, manifest, measurements derived from geometry, or the
implementation method.

This is a gate for freezing S12 solely as a **provisional head receiver**. It
is not approval of the standalone neutral sculpt or permission to ignore a
visible interface defect.

## Six-view findings

### Symmetry

The front and rear silhouettes are bilaterally balanced: crown center,
shoulder widths, cheek extrema, lower-corner heights, and the shallow bottom
arc do not show a material left/right form mismatch. The opposed profiles have
matching gross depth, vertical extent, crown plane, and lower corner turns.

The underside value pattern is not symmetric under the directional light: one
profile and one three-quarter view show a much darker recessed band than their
opposites. The outer silhouettes remain consistent enough that this reads as
the known underside/shading interface defect rather than evidence of a second
large asymmetric head mass. It must still be tested geometrically when the
collar is fitted.

### Rear, profiles, and three-quarter consistency

- The rear keeps the same broad, shallow-crown cushion envelope as the front;
  it does not reveal a rear point, dome, or extra bulge.
- Both profiles remain shallow and near parallel through the central height.
  Neither side expands into a sphere or collapses into a thin card.
- Both three-quarter views interpolate coherently between the broad front/rear
  envelope and the shallow profiles. The cheek-to-side transition is smooth,
  without a detached lobe or unexpected corner.
- The underside is the exception: the darker three-quarter view exposes a
  folded-looking shelf/wedge beneath the otherwise continuous cushion mass.
  This is the same known interface failure seen in profile, not a passed
  construction cue.

Rear evidence in the canonical references is fully covered by hair, so the
clean rear silhouette here is only a provisional receiver constraint. It is
not evidence for an authored rear seam or final visible rear construction.

## Defect-category checks

| Defect | Six-view judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Egg/sphere | **No.** Depth remains shallow and the central profile planes stay near parallel. |
| Human jaw | **No.** Lower cheeks remain broad; the chin does not taper anatomically. |
| Severe box | **No as a hidden receiver; yes as an unresolved final-medium tendency.** Corners and crown are softened, but the profiles still read as a smooth shallow block without sewn-panel definition. |
| Left/right silhouette mismatch | **No material mismatch.** Lighting reveals the underside differently, but gross bounds agree. |
| Hidden underside fold/cavity | **Yes, unresolved.** Most obvious in the darker profile and three-quarter view. |

## Receiver-freeze verdict

**S12 can be frozen solely as a provisional head receiver for collar and hair
integration.** The six views do not expose a macro silhouette contradiction
that requires another isolated whole-head edit before integration. Its broad
cheeks, restrained crown, shallow depth, and non-anatomical chin are stable
enough to locate the collar, hair cap, face opening, fringe, side panels, and
bow roots.

This verdict is deliberately narrow:

- freeze the visible S12 envelope above the lowest interface band;
- use hair to validate the complete front, rear, profile, and three-quarter
  outer mass against the references;
- do not call S12 a finished plush head or a passed neutral sculpt; and
- do not add seams, fuzz, wrinkles, or other detail to disguise the remaining
  construction issue.

## Explicit underside delegation contract

The underside may be delegated to the collar interface only under all of
these conditions:

1. The collar fully covers the recessed dark band in every fixed view and in
   the intended seated-pose range.
2. The evaluated head and collar surfaces do not cross, float, or create an
   accidental visible gap.
3. Contact reads as one broad, softly compressed seating region rather than
   two bright corner contacts around a hollow center.
4. The collar can satisfy those conditions without moving the frozen cheek,
   crown, face-plane, or profile extrema.

If any condition fails, the underside must be reopened as a bounded
interface-specific correction. Delegation records ownership; it does not
convert the cavity into accepted geometry.
