# A85 S12 implementation-blind pixel review

## Review isolation

I inspected only the pinned S12 and S08 fixed-camera front/profile pixels, the
supplied canonical front and turntable views, and the controlling bare-cushion
measurements. I did not inspect Blend geometry, topology, scripts, object
names, manifests, or the implementation method.

This review makes two separate decisions: whether the crown correction is
visibly safe, and whether S12 is stable enough to serve as a **provisional
integration foundation**. It is not a sculpt-approval review.

## Crown correction

**Keep the S12 crown correction.** The fixed-view difference is confined to
the intended upper perimeter:

- In front, the crown is lower and more restrained while retaining a broad,
  shallow arc. It no longer contributes unnecessary dome height beneath the
  hair and bow envelope.
- In profile, the top plane is shallower and more nearly level, with soft
  corner turns. It reads more like the top of a shallow cushion than a rounded
  helmet.
- Maximum cheek breadth, lower-cheek widening, the shallow frontal chin arc,
  total profile depth, and the lower profile silhouette do not visibly move.
- The change introduces no egg, human-jaw, box, or pinched-crown regression.

S12 is therefore a visible, bounded improvement over S08 rather than merely a
numeric edit.

## Provisional foundation verdict

**Yes: S12 is safe to freeze as the provisional head foundation for collar
and hair integration.** This means it is the best current interface candidate,
not that the bare cushion passes final approval.

The front envelope now provides a stable broad cheek band, restrained crown,
and soft chin arc. The profile retains the documented shallow depth and
near-parallel face/rear planes instead of becoming an egg. Those are the
silhouettes that the collar, hair cap, fringe opening, side locks, and bow must
fit. Additional isolated crown iteration would now have less value than
testing whether those covering parts reproduce the controlling composite
silhouette.

Recommended next action is to freeze S12's visible envelope and add only the
first collar/hair integration owners around it. Do not alter the head cushion
while diagnosing the resulting complete hair-and-face mass.

## Absolute status

S12 remains **rejected for standalone approval**. It is an intentionally bare,
smooth component and still lacks the sewn-panel, attachment, and complete
identity evidence required by the absolute gate. Provisional integration
safety does not relax those requirements.

| Decision | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Crown correction | **Keep** |
| Best current bare-head survivor | **S12** |
| Safe provisional collar/hair foundation | **Yes** |
| Standalone sculpt approval | **No** |

## Known hidden underside interface

The profile still shows the previously documented dark recessed underside
band. This review does **not** declare it fixed. Because the canonical plush
hides this region at the collar, it may be acceptable as a provisional hidden
interface only if the collar covers it cleanly and the evaluated surfaces do
not cross.

Track it separately during collar fitting:

- no dark cavity may remain visible outside the collar opening;
- the collar and head surfaces must not clip or produce an accidental gap;
- the head must seat with broad soft contact rather than on two bright lower
  corner points; and
- any later underside repair must preserve the now-frozen S12 front/profile
  envelope above the lowest interface band.

If collar integration exposes the cavity or cannot establish clean contact,
reopen the underside as an interface-specific correction. Its hidden status
is not evidence that the geometry is approved.
