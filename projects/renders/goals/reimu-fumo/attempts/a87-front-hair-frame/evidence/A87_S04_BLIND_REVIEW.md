# A87 S04 implementation-blind three-piece review

## Scope and evidence

I reviewed only the S04 front and mirrored three-quarter pixels against the
canonical front, canonical 180-degree turn, clean front, physical front and
side, sofa turn, and supplied turn GIF. I treated S04 explicitly as a bounded
three-piece module: one front fringe panel and two side locks. The crown and
rear cap are intentionally absent and are **not** counted as defects.

The canonical front controls the frontal fringe identity. The canonical turn
controls thickness, overlap, and three-quarter construction. Supporting
physical references were used to judge whether the neutral forms could
plausibly be sewn fabric panels.

## Verdict

**Preserve S04 as a provisional integration module.** It is the first reviewed
A87 candidate whose representation is compatible with the reference
construction. The frontal piece now reads as a shallow fabric sheet with a
visible free edge, while both side locks read as separately owned tapered
leaves rather than capsules. The broad root, long central wedge, unequal side
lobes, and front-to-lock depth order are recognizably aligned with the Reimu
Fumo references.

This is a provisional-module pass, not standalone sculpt approval. The module
cannot pass the final absolute gate until its upper root is covered and seated
by the deliberately missing crown/rear hair construction and the complete
hair/face mass is reviewed in all fixed views.

approval claim but do not justify discarding this structurally useful part.

## At most two small contour refinements

1. **Make the two lock tips slightly blunter and less needle-like.** Broaden
   only the final short segment and give the pair subtly different endpoint
   angles, preserving their current length, thin profile, roots, and
   front-to-back placement. The physical front and turntable show cut fabric
   ends rather than teardrop points.
2. **Narrow the central fringe wedge modestly at its lower third.** Preserve
   its current reach and the asymmetric neighboring lobes; reduce only the
   bulbous lower width so the endpoint reads as the reference's folded central
   flap rather than a rounded tongue.

Both refinements are optional local contour edits. Do not rebuild the panel,
inflate it, add detail to compensate, or alter the frozen head receiver. If
either change weakens the present face-versus-edge read in a three-quarter
view, retain S04 unchanged and proceed to the separate crown/rear integration
module.
