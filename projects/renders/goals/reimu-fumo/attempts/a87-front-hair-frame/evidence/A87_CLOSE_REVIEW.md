renders for S04 and S07 against every tracked Reimu Fumo reference. The

## Rejection evidence for S07

S07 changes the wrong macro category. The references show compact side locks
that frame the cheeks while the broader front/rear hair mass carries the head
silhouette. S07 turns those locks into the dominant vertical forms: their tips
approach the underside of the head, the face opening becomes a tall narrow
channel, and their inward lean visually competes with the central fringe. The
result is more like long character hair than a sewn Fumo panel assembly.

The stronger root continuity in S07 does not compensate for this identity and
proportion regression. Because the failure is visible in front and both
three-quarters, S07 must not replace the S04 checkpoint.

## Close action

Freeze S04 exactly at the recorded source hash and close A87 with it as the
provisional front-hair module. Do not blend S07's long-lock geometry back into
the survivor. Any later root seating adjustment belongs to the bounded
crown/rear integration interface and must preserve S04's current short lock
lengths, lateral placement, fringe asymmetry, shallow panel faces, and visible
front-over-side depth order.
