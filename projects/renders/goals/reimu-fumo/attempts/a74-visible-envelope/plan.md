## Process reset

A72 and A73 prove that a mechanically valid hidden receiver can satisfy every
numeric gate and still produce a rounded mattress/egg. The references do not
expose that receiver. They do expose the brown construction that owns the bad
pixels. A74 therefore starts from observed visible boundaries and fits a
subordinate support inside them; it does not derive another visible shell from
an unobserved support.

## Primary-owned decision review

The strongest objection is that scripted visible panels may repeat the old
card, visor, or helmet failures. A closed shell is rejected by A72/A73, flat
extrusions are rejected by A34/A46/A70, incremental rung-003 deformation is
rejected by A68/A69, and the available synthetic sculpt interface cannot make
broad reliable edits. The smallest materially different, reversible test is
an open curved saddle plus independent lightly filled pockets, constrained in
front and both profile/three-quarter projections before detail.

Verdict: proceed in two gates. P0 owns only the front/crown/temple field, two
cheek locks, and hidden face support. P1 may add two rear pockets only after P0
passes. Stop immediately if coherent coverage requires closing the saddle or
extending it into another helmet.

## P0 — open saddle, locks, and subordinate support

1. Copy the observed curve and scanline artifacts into one machine-readable
   contract. Static preflight verifies their values and source hashes; the
   builder consumes that same contract.
2. Build `A74_CrownTemple_Saddle` as one sparse, curved, open subdivision patch
   with a source-traced asymmetric lower hairline. It has thin paired skins
   only where necessary for fabric thickness; it has no closed rear wall.
3. Build non-mirrored `A74_Left_Cheek_Lock` and
   `A74_Right_Cheek_Lock` as independent, lightly filled two-skin pockets with
   free lower edges and narrow edge strips.
4. Fit `A74_Subordinate_Face_Support` behind the visible field. It may be seen
   only through the accepted beige opening and must contribute zero pixels to
   other fixed views.
5. Render front, both profiles, both front three-quarters, and a rear support
   leak mask in frozen whole-subject context before any rear leaf, seam,
   material, fiber, rig, or bow reseating work.

P0 passes only if critical hairline/aperture landmarks are within `.03 Wh`,
other front extrema within `.05 Wh`, beige exposure is `.603 +/- .03 Wh`,
profile compact-field depth stays inside `.77--.85 Wh`, support leakage is
zero, and blind scores for identity/construction/contact are all at least
6/10. Any helmet, box, mask, visor, card, bridge, bald gap, clipping, or need
for a closed saddle is an immediate reset.
