## Decision question

but worsened the lower assembly's cape/ramp category, what is the smallest
causally distinct work unit most likely to create acceptance-visible progress?
Does the evidence support continuing the goal, changing authoring modality,
switching modules, or declaring the current approach infeasible?

1. Simplified native Blender sculpt observation and one small sculpt coupon on

2. Another direct coordinate edit of rung003's lower stack, including the seat

3. A newly constructed/replacement lower module using closed stuffed support

4. Preserve rung003 and switch to another bounded high-impact module such as

5. Stop model work as infeasible under current authoring interfaces.

## Evidence and method

- Inspect A96's exact baseline/candidate pixels, edit implementation, and
  structural receipts, not only its summary.
- Reconcile A96 with A68/A71 scripted sculpt, A76 live-interface, A94 XTest,
  A95 strategy, and historical high-water evidence.
- Identify what A96 actually falsified: parameter choice, inherited topology,
  analytical surface mapping, the whole direct-edit modality, or the complete
  goal.
- Compare expected time-to-first-pixel, modeling leverage, distinctness from
  failed attempts, animation preservation, and hard stop conditions.
- Route exactly one fresh implementation-independent reviewer; the coordinator
  owns the final verdict and may override only with explicit evidence.

## Acceptance

Close only with one executable next work unit whose exact input, operation,
output, first-pixel gate, stop rule, and animation-preservation boundary are
specified. Reject repetition disguised as new parameters. If no causally
distinct bounded route remains, say so explicitly and identify the concrete
external capability required to resume.
