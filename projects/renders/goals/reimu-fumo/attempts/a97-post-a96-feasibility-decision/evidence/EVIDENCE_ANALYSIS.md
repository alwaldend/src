# A97 coordinator evidence analysis

## What A96 established

A96 was valid as a rendered candidate and a fast rejection, but it did not
execute the authoring discriminator its plan claimed. It loaded exact rung003,
saved reversible shape keys, preserved structural digests, and rendered valid
complete-subject pixels. Its visible result improved foot height but worsened
the side/three-quarter cape and floor rail.

The implementation matters. `build_candidate.py` did not make a few sparse
Edit-mode or proportional edits. It defined dense `front_surface`,
`rear_surface`, and `side_surface` formulas, remapped nearly every free vertex
across the garment owners, moved ruffles and stitches to those formulas, and
changed 30 of the 31 allowed owners. The internal seat pad—the intended closed
support—was not changed at all. The rear hem moved from about `Y=.049 m` to
`Y=.071 m` while the unchanged seat support ended near `Y=.040 m`. A diagonal
unsupported roof was therefore the expected geometric output.

## What it falsified

A96 falsifies its specific dense analytical row-map and the hypothesis that
increasing rear hem extent creates seated volume. It adds another failure to
the historical parametric-sheet family. It does **not** falsify rung003's
topology, a truly sparse direct edit, native sculpting, or the complete goal,
because those operations were not actually performed.

This corrects the coordinator's initial overclaim in the first draft of this
analysis. The pixel reset remains correct; the causal scope is narrower.

## Historical cross-check

Earlier parametric surfaces, subdivision, rectangular cloth simulation,
macro-drape correction, and hidden-support variants repeatedly created capes.
That strongly rejects another generated or rectangular-sheet lower mapping.
It does not close artist-driven local deformation: scripted sculpt strokes
produced only dimples, A76 had no pointer provider, and A94's one real XTest
drag produced no post geometry because its competing modal sentinel stalled
the session. Native pointer sculpt therefore remains materially unobserved.

## Alternatives

### Another scripted lower mapping or replacement

Reject. Different exponents, hem distances, simulation time, or generator
resolution would repeat the same analytical family.

### Claim that sparse/direct editing or inherited topology failed

Reject the claim. A96's dense 30-owner formula remap did not test it. A future
model-bearing attempt may still use a small local edit, but only after the
missing authoring-control discriminator is resolved.

### Switch to hair, bow, or sleeves now

Defer. Those modules share the same missing broad-form authoring capability.
Switching now would move the bottleneck rather than answer it.

### Declare the goal infeasible now

Reject. A94 already proved that XTest can deliver a real pointer drag to the
mapped Blender window, but its sentinel and internal screenshot invalidated
observation. A stripped result-bearing path remains available.

## Process finding

A96's early pixels prevented a long refinement cycle, but the coordinator did
not detect that the author had replaced the planned sparse method with a dense
generator before execution. Future work must gate the actual operation, not
only its source hash and output structure. For a small-module plan, an author
must publish the proposed edit mechanism before the first mutation; the
coordinator either approves that mechanism or returns early when its interface
is wrong.
