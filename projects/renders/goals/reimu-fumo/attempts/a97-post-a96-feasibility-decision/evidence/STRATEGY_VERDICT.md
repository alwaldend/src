### Pass gate

Pass only if all hold:

- live before/after captures are valid, non-black, and consistently framed;
- at least 8% of vertices move more than 0.10 mm and at least 1% move more than
  0.50 mm;
- at least 90% of materially moved vertices form one connected component;
- displacement is predominantly in world `+Y`, maximum displacement is above
  0.75 mm and below 6 mm, and distant vertices remain unaffected;
- the fixed right view changes at least 1% of subject pixels, with a broad
  connected silhouette arc rather than a dimple, hard ring, pinch, tear, or
  global affine shift;
- the three-quarter view corroborates the same broad deformation; and
- target topology/matrix and all source/non-target coordinates remain exact.

### Stop and consequence

Stop before input on an invalid external baseline. Stop after the sole drag on
missing post evidence, zero/local/wrong-direction effect, category failure,
topology/source drift, or any timeout. Do not repair or retry A98.

A pass authorizes a separate model-bearing incremental sculpt attempt in exact
rung003 context; it is not likeness or acceptance evidence itself. A failure
establishes current autonomous authoring infeasibility. Resumption would then
require a stable real-pointer/live-sculpt capability or human DCC authoring,
not another analytical generator.
