# Reimu Fumo end-to-end process audit

## Scope and evidence boundary

This review covers the imported A0--A67 history, structured attempts A68--A89,
and the visible artifacts surviving after A89. The canonical boundary is goal
resource version 54: outcome `open`, execution `paused`, no accepted attempt,
and no accepted result digest. The audit does not treat command success,
topology checks, hashes, or an isolated module keep as whole-result progress.

Primary evidence:

- `projects/renders/goals/reimu-fumo/{goal.yaml,criteria.yaml,README.md}`;
- every A68--A89 `attempt.yaml`, `plan.md`, `result.md`, and retained evidence;
- imported history under
  `projects/renders/goals/reimu-fumo/attempts/imported-unversioned/evidence/`;
- the current A87, A88, and rejected A89 packets under
  `out/reimu_fumo_attempt_083_incremental_sculpt/`; and
- the best complete but rejected rung-003 comparison under
  `out/reimu_fumo_working_ladder/`.

Canonical attempt wall spans include pauses, user interruptions, and idle
overnight time. They are useful for elapsed-process accounting, not a claim
about continuous labor.

## Executive verdict

The workflow became very good at protecting files, rejecting bad candidates,
and documenting why they failed. It did not become good at producing the
requested plush.

The current bottleneck is not Blender throughput, the sandbox, the MCP port,
RAM, or the number of agent slots. By A82, author start to a controlling pair
was 3m35.150s and the categorical decision arrived at 6m11.794s. By A87, live
edits took seconds and a pinned three-view packet took about 18 seconds. Yet
the latest A89 artifact is still a blank rounded head with plate-like hair;
its rear is a rigid cape with exposed receiver regions. The failure is now
the modeling hypothesis, visual decomposition, and timing of artistic review.

The decisive process error is optimizing locally valid modules without
re-establishing a viable whole-subject parent. A85, A87, and A88 are real
provisional interface gains, but they have never been shown to compose into a
recognizable complete Reimu Fumo. A89 immediately repeated the historical
cape/fin failure. Continuing to add modules to that lineage without a
whole-context bakeoff is sunk-cost escalation.

**Decision-review verdict: revise.** Preserve A85/A87/A88 byte-exact as an
immutable control and parts bank, but stop A89 tuning and stop treating the
head-only lineage as the presumptive parent. The next plan must make a complete
construction-aware plush blockout and a whole-context A88 salvage control
compete on the same fixed views before more detailed hair, rig, material, or
promotion work.

### Legacy A0--A67

The legacy archive shows the same structural pattern at a larger scale:

- Attempts 0--9 contain more than 40 internal variants/experiments and no
  accepted model geometry. Attempts 1 and 2 accepted only repository migration
  and reference calibration.
- Attempts 10--19 contain 11 explicit decision cycles, all rejected; five
  successive preflight families A15--A19 produced no candidate pixels.
- The imported failure ledger counts 200 overlapping defect occurrences:
  head 31, human proportions 11, flat/disconnected construction 46, hair 23,
  bow 11, clipping 12, feet 11, weak side/rear 50, expression 2, and bland
  material/contact 3. A65/A66 recurrences are explicitly excluded, so these
  are a lower bound.
- Only two narrow historical modules ever achieved strong isolated evidence:
  the A25 support cushion and A28 rear felt panel. Integration later reopened
  the support's crown/temple interface. No complete head, whole plush, or
  reusable rig was accepted.

The structured A68--A89 loop therefore did not encounter a new problem. It
continued a long history in which mechanical/numeric validity repeatedly
preceded and failed the first absolute visual gate.

## What genuinely improved

These are useful infrastructure or modeling facts and should be retained.

1. **Pinned render and verification path.** Repository-pinned Blender 5.2.1,
   fixed cameras, immutable snapshots, clean reopen, and exact hashes reliably
   separate authoring from verification. A70 measured roughly 33 seconds for a
   five-view packet; A87 measured about 18 seconds for three views.
2. **Reference dossier.** The canonical 25 cm front and all 30 frames of the
   180-degree turn are tracked with explicit authority. Supporting physical
   front/side and construction references are retained instead of averaged
   into one impossible view.
3. **Absolute rejection improved.** Implementation-blind reviews repeatedly
   rejected technically clean helmets, cards, eggs, mattresses, and capes.
   Owner-ID passes in A83/A88 correctly separated coverage from construction.
4. **Source safety.** Isolated copies, one `.blend` writer, frozen hashes, and
   no mutation of the Sisyphus scene prevented rejected experiments from
   damaging the tracked asset.
5. **A85 receiver fact.** S12 is a shallow, measured receiver without the old
   egg/human-jaw silhouette. It is a useful hidden support candidate, not an
   approved head.
6. **A87/A88 ownership facts.** A87 S04 preserves a short fringe plus two
   cheek locks; A88 S07 preserves a two-panel crown and closes the apex leak
   without changing the protected front zone (`IoU 1.0`). These are plausible
   parts, not additive proof of a complete head.

## Why user-visible progress stayed low

### 1. The work unit stopped being the requested subject

Rung 003 is visibly poor, but it is a complete character and can be judged as
Reimu. A84 deliberately started a clean head-only scene; A85--A89 then refined
receiver, collar, fringe, crown, and rear hair in increasing isolation. The
current artifact has no face, expression, bow, body, garments, feet, material,
pose, or complete silhouette. It cannot demonstrate progress on the user's
actual image even when a local interface improves.

This violates the result-oriented meaning of small modules: retain enough
whole-result context to expose composition and interface harm. A87 itself
proved the problem when context-free image-space lock measurements produced
detached, long anime strands. The same lesson was not elevated into an
obligatory whole-character integration packet.

### 2. Analytical generation remained the default for organic form

The history repeatedly used `from_pydata` graphs, parametric lofts,
superellipsoids, radial fields, Solidify, cloth pressure, and generated panel
cages to author forms whose acceptance depended on soft, irregular sewn
construction. Their recurring outputs were predictable: helmets, mattresses,
cards, fins, paddles, cones, and capes.

A68/A71 showed that synthetic scripted sculpt strokes did not provide broad
control; A76 rechecked the same limitation and produced no candidate. A84--A85
finally changed to an interactive/direct sparse cage, and A85 produced the
first useful recent receiver. That improvement is evidence for direct visual
control, not a reason to return to broad generated panels. A89's three-panel
generator promptly returned to a rigid shield.

### 3. Decomposition repeatedly chose the wrong pixel owner

Examples:

- A73 optimized an unobserved hidden receiver and never reached its mandatory
  visible-layer coupon; its score regressed from A72's 5/10 to 4/10.
- A74 hid rear owners while requiring a rear gate, guaranteeing baldness.
- A75 changed a receiver visible in only about 28% of front, 16% of 3Q, 4% of
  profile, and 0% of rear while the rejected hair shell owned the defect.
- A86 asked a compact central collar to occlude a laterally wider head. Its own
  result proved the collar would have to become a head-width hard yoke.
- A89 closed literal gaps by merging three leaves into one shield, confusing
  geometric coverage with manufactured construction.

The modules were often small in object count but not high-leverage in pixels.

### 4. Review was independent but too late to control authoring

Blind review was usually accurate; timing was the problem.

- A70 performed three refinements after the first packet already exposed the
  absent shared rest surface.
- A79 waited 94m30s for pixels after extensive contract and builder work.
- A80's rejected pairs sat 3m21.159s and 1m57.556s before self-review.
- A88 spent three diagnoses on one apex slit before owner-ID pixels and a
  single-variable clearance test identified receiver breakthrough.
- A89 authored S00--S06 before the first implementation-blind review applied
  the dominant cape/fin veto.

A82 is the correct counterexample: reviewer, immutable snapshot, and pinned
pair were on the critical path, and the wrong atomic shell died within seven
minutes.

### 5. Later acceptance tiers displaced failed appearance work

A83 began as one incremental hair deformation but expanded through C1--C19,
hair and sleeve replacements, a nine-control rig, four actions, and R1/R2
deformation scaffolding. Its canonical evidence set contains 18 documents.
The retained appearance C1b still failed likeness and plush construction, yet
rig criterion work proceeded. The A83 close result later admitted that too many
generated hair/sleeve cycles ran after the coupled macro scaffold had become
the dominant failure.

Reusable structure is valuable, but rigging a rejected appearance is not on
the result critical path. The reference-fidelity skill explicitly orders macro
silhouette and construction before materials, microdetail, and later technical
acceptance.

### 6. Documentation became activity rather than control

The goal record is strongest when it binds a concise hypothesis, artifact, and
decision. It became process theater when it elaborated a hypothesis before
pixels or restated old evidence.

- A74 accumulated roughly 392 lines of design review and 393 lines of topology
  algorithm before a categorically bad first image.
- A74--A76 retained 2,909 lines for two rejected visual candidates and one
  unmodified interface probe.
- A79's own process checkpoint said "pixels next," then 64 more files appeared
  before the first geometry pixel.
- The 1,261-line noncanonical artifact log still says goal RV51/A88 active,
  while the canonical goal is RV54/A89 closed/paused.
- The 2,404-line legacy `current_attempt.md` still says A68 is active.

These stale duplicate projections make resumption and user inspection harder.
The generated canonical goal projection should own current state; one short
artifact index should own clickable current visuals. Legacy history should not
pretend to be current.

### 7. Tool and setup churn recurred after the path was solved

The Flatpak/pinned split was legitimate because the user selected Flatpak for
live authoring and pinned Blender for verification. Its repeated operational
experimentation was not:

- A76 spent a canonical attempt proving the MCP lacked real pointer events
  after A68/A71 had already falsified the synthetic stroke path.
- A81 tried foreground Eevee; it produced no PNG after 120 seconds, while the
  pinned fallback rendered the pair in 17.906 seconds.
- A82 inherited a wedged Flatpak host, restart/reconnect failure on
  `localhost:9876`, and at least 5m28.053s of pre-clock setup latency.
- A83 rendered snapshots redundantly and cancelled a Flatpak preview after it
  exceeded the feedback budget.
- A80 duplicated one-off runners: 796 lines of build/render scripts plus 151
  lines of compositors, despite A79 already identifying a reusable preview
  consumer as the next requirement.

The proven rule is simple: author in one live host only when direct UI context
is needed, save one immutable snapshot, and render it once through the pinned
batch path. More MCP, launcher, renderer, or Bazel infrastructure is not the
next leverage point.

### 8. Parallelism exceeded integration capacity, then was blamed as scarce

A79 ran at least 13 workstreams and produced roughly 399 files before one
diagnostic sheet. Reference extraction, immutable alternative copies, and
blind review were useful; simultaneous schema, material, hull, interface,
density, variant, and builder work created a review queue and a serial
authorization choke.

One monolithic artistic candidate needs one Blender author and fast review.
Useful parallel lanes are bounded: one alternative whole-form hypothesis, one
reference/overlay worker, and one implementation-blind reviewer. Twenty agent
slots cannot accelerate the coordinator's visual decision and may delay it.

### 9. Provisional progress is not durably preserved

The exact A87 S04, A88 S07, and A89 S06 Blend files are all ignored by the
repository-wide `out/` rule. Their goal records retain hashes and local paths,
but no deterministic regeneration recipe. The project goal declares durable
retention, while the actual provisional geometry can disappear with scratch.

That does not justify promoting unapproved geometry into the final asset. It
does require a deliberate durable checkpoint mechanism for an exact survivor,
under Git LFS or another retained artifact store, before claiming the work is
safe across machine or scratch loss.

## Useful infrastructure versus process theater

| Keep | Why it earns its cost |
| --- | --- |
| Tracked canonical references and all 30 turn frames | They control actual dimensions, layer order, and variant identity. |
| One pinned batch renderer and fixed camera spec | It produces decision pixels in 18--33 seconds and cleanly verifies snapshots. |
| One live authoring host when required | It enables direct edits without making the deliverable depend on host state. |
| Immutable candidate copies and protected hashes | They prevent accidental regression and permit honest undo. |
| Immediate beauty pair plus implementation-blind veto | It detects the repeated wrong-category defects cheaply. |
| Component-ID pass after a visible interface failure | A88 proves it can distinguish coverage, gap, and breakthrough. |
| One canonical goal record and concise attempt delta | It preserves decisions and avoids false acceptance. |

| Stop or move off the critical path | Why it is process theater now |
| --- | --- |
| Production schemas, topology contracts, and promotion checks before first pixels | A79 proved they delayed a 29-second visual veto by 94 minutes. |
| New one-off builders and compositors | The invariant runner already exists; payload variation is the work. |
| Full packet, hashes, and archival report for an obvious first-pair failure | They cannot change the decision. |
| Rig, actions, deformation systems, materials, or microdetail before likeness/construction pass | They improve a rejected subject, not the requested result. |
| More agents assigned to adjacent speculative branches | A79 proved fanout can exceed review capacity. |
| Duplicate current-state prose and session logs | They became stale and conflict with the canonical goal. |
| Another MCP/sculpt capability probe without a new input mechanism | The missing real pointer path is already established. |

## Skill-compliance audit

### Goal skill

**Followed:** versioned canonical records; exact bindings; one coordinator;
honest `reset`/`refine`; immutable closed attempts; source protection; deep
reviews at A73, A77, A79/A80, and A83; isolated worker copies; explicit pause.

**Not followed consistently:** result priority; small causal-reach checks;
minimum whole-result context; first decision-bearing artifact budget;
standing review on the first risky probe; changing the hypothesis after the
same stable defect survived twice; stopping off-critical workers; durable
retention of provisional survivors. A79 is the clearest breach: the process
recorded the correct "pixels next" control but did not enforce it.

### Repository Blender skill

**Followed:** pinned Blender for batch/deliverable evidence; explicit Flatpak
exception for the requested live host; task-owned `out/`; frozen snapshot
handoff; clean reopen; explicit cameras/resolution; protected source hashes.

**Not followed consistently:** analytical generation was used as the default
for organic constructed forms; foreground/live rendering was retried after the
batch path was proven; snapshots were sometimes rendered twice; capability
investigations repeated known failures. The skill's direct-edit guidance began
to work only around A84/A85.

### Blender reference-fidelity skill

**Followed:** authoritative multi-view references, normalized `Wh` landmarks,
fixed cameras, absolute blind review, wrong-category vetoes, and undo of failed
geometry.

**Not followed consistently:** no current per-asset scorecard was maintained
after 2026-08-28; many recent cycles used unaligned contact sheets instead of a
registered overlay/edge difference; head-only modules lacked minimum
whole-subject context; reviewer timing lagged authoring; later technical tiers
ran while macro likeness and plush construction still failed. A89's blind
review explicitly notes that its views were not camera-calibrated overlays and
that numerical landmark tolerances remained unverified.

## Controls the recovery plan should enforce

1. The progress unit is a recognizable complete neutral plush, not a local
   module, topology pass, or command receipt.
2. Run two isolated lanes only: a fresh complete construction-aware low-detail
   blockout and one immutable A88 whole-context salvage control.
3. One Blender author per lane. Freeze front plus worst three-quarter within a
   measured 15--30 minute authoring budget; the pinned render itself should
   stay near its demonstrated seconds-scale latency.
4. An implementation-blind reviewer judges that first pair immediately. Allow
   at most one causally named correction. Do not author S00--S06 before review.
5. Require whole-subject recognition, silhouette, construction, and identity
   at a defensible internal threshold before six views, detailed panels,
   materials, retopology, or rig work. This does not weaken the final 8/10
   approval gate.
6. Use all relevant references with one controller per dimension and aligned
   overlays. Do not execute context-free image-space deltas literally.
7. Preserve A85/A87/A88 as evidence, not as privileged parents. A blind A/B
   chooses the simpler viable whole result; a tie does not favor sunk cost.
8. Keep the canonical goal projection current and replace the stale artifact
   log with one short link index. Retain exact surviving geometry durably only
   after it earns provisional parent status.
9. Do not touch the final reusable asset, materials stage, pose, integration,
   or Sisyphus scene until the standalone neutral whole-plush parent passes its
   explicit approval gate.

## Bottom line

The process did learn how to render, verify, reject, and preserve evidence.
It has not yet learned how to choose and visually control a whole plush form.
Recent lack of progress is not mysterious: the loop optimized partial owners,
mechanics, and records while the subject-level hypothesis disappeared from the
artifact. Recovery requires fewer concurrent hypotheses, earlier pixels,
directly controllable whole-form authoring, immediate blind vetoes, and a
whole-plush comparison before any further module investment.
