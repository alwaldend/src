# Reimu Fumo recovery plan after A90

## Objective

Restore acceptance-visible progress by selecting one viable complete
whole-plush parent before spending more work on isolated hair, detail,
materials, retopology, or rigging. Preserve all previous candidates and the
tracked model; every new branch begins from an isolated immutable copy or an
empty task-owned scene.

This plan does not weaken the final goal. Its 6/10 and 7/10 thresholds are
internal parent-selection gates, not user approval or completion.

## Invariant execution loop

Use the existing repository-pinned Blender 5.2.1 packet renderer. A user-
requested live Flatpak/MCP process may author disposable geometry, but it is
never the renderer, verifier, or deliverable toolchain. Set all configurable
host scratch and temporary paths explicitly under one task-specific `out/`
directory.

For every authoring branch:

1. One author owns one isolated `.blend`; no shared live mutation.
2. Freeze and hash the snapshot before rendering.
3. Render the canonical front and the worst three-quarter first at 512 px.
4. An implementation-blind reviewer sees the references and pixels before the
   authoring method, intent, metrics, or previous candidate.
5. Reject immediately on a wrong category: human anatomy, egg/sphere, helmet,
   mask, card, cape, cone dress, tube limbs, floating primitives, bald support,
   or major clipping.
6. Permit at most one named causally reachable correction after the first
   packet. A second miss closes the branch.
7. Render six views and component IDs only after the controlling pair passes.
8. A local module may be retained only if the same snapshot also improves a
   whole-head or whole-character context packet.

Target first decision-bearing pixels within 30 minutes of active geometry
start and within 60 seconds of geometry freeze. If the first target is missed,
stop expanding the work unit and render the smallest safe existing probe.

## Attempt A91 — fresh complete construction-aware blockout

### Hypothesis

A complete sparse character makes identity, proportion, contact, and wrong-
category failures visible early enough to select a viable parent, while
construction-aware ownership avoids repeating the old balls/cones/boxes
blockouts.

### Authoring boundary

- Start in an empty task-owned scene with the frozen cameras, 25 cm scale, and
  reference registration.
- Do not append rejected visible geometry.
- Author the complete seated plush at low resolution: shallow cushion head,
  gross hair volume, bow mass and tails, compact torso/hip mass, skirt/hem,
  two detached sleeve masses, and two small stuffed foot pods.
- Use one or two sparse deformable owners per major mass. Shapes must have
  volume, soft overlap, and plausible contact; “low detail” does not authorize
  disconnected primitives.
- Use simple owner colors only. No ruffles, stitches, fibers, final eye
  graphics, simulation, retopology, armature, or materials.

### Gate

Freeze front/three-quarter within 30 minutes. One correction is allowed. Keep
only if:

- an unlabeled reviewer recognizes the canonical Reimu variant at blockout
  level;
- overall likeness, macro silhouette, and identity are each at least 6/10;
- main front landmarks are provisionally within `0.08 Wh` and broad depth
  bands pass;
- the head is broader than the compact seated body, feet remain small and
  partly occluded, and bow/hair establish the correct silhouette; and
- no major wrong-category defect makes refinement implausible.

Otherwise reset the representation, not its detail.

## Attempt A92 — A88 whole-context salvage control

Run A92 concurrently with A91 on a separate exact copy.

### Hypothesis

The weak A88 subject read may be caused mainly by missing identity/body
context; a cheap complete packet can decide whether the provisional modules
deserve further work.

### Authoring boundary

- Copy exact A88 S07.
- Keep A85 S12, A87 S04, and A88 S07 byte-equivalent.
- Add disposable proportionally registered proxies for face graphics, gross
  rear-hair volume, bow, collar/body, skirt, sleeves, and feet.
- Do not repair A88 and do not append any A89 geometry.
- Use the same cameras, framing, lighting, and owner colors as A91.

### Gate

Run absolute reviews first, then a randomized A/B against A91 and rung 003.
Keep the modular lineage only if it clearly beats A91 on complete-subject
likeness, silhouette, and construction, reads as the exact variant without a
label, and has no major cap/helmet or exposed-receiver failure. A tie selects
A91 because it has fewer inherited interfaces and less sunk-cost risk.

If A88 loses, retain its pieces as a parts/reference bank only; do not continue
its lower-rear hair.

## Attempt A93 — one macro correction or authoring-control test

If A91 or A92 survives:

- freeze the winner as explicitly unaccepted;
- name its single largest whole-image defect;
- identify the smallest coupled owner that can move a meaningful fraction of
  those pixels without breaking protected views;
- make one reversible correction and compare the exact same controlling and
  regression-risk views; and
- keep only a visible improvement with no new major defect and whole-subject
  scores moving toward 7/10.

If neither survives, do not generate another analytic model. Test the direct
sculpt input path for 10-15 minutes on a disposable coupon. It must perform
three broad, independently directed deformations that each move a meaningful
low-frequency silhouette region without dimples, zero-effect output, or
camera-only illusion. Only a passing coupon authorizes one complete low-
resolution manual sculpt. A failed coupon closes that modality.

Two misses in the same subject-level category trigger a new strategy review,
not a fifth parameter family.

## Attempt A94 — constructed-form and animation-readiness coupon

Run only if A93 leaves a complete neutral parent with no major macro failure.

- Convert one identity-critical region (prefer the complete hair/head
  interface) and one motion-critical region (one sleeve/torso or hip/skirt
  interface) into clean manufactured topology.
- Preserve the winning outer pixels while adding real thickness, roots,
  overlap, seam allowances, and deformation-friendly flow.
- Add only enough armature to test a small head turn and one arm motion.
- Do not texture the full character.

Pass the recovery phase only if the constructed copy holds the accepted macro
silhouette within `0.03 Wh`, has no floating or clipping surface in required
views, and the test motions do not tear the two interfaces. Otherwise undo the
local topology/rig change rather than rebuilding the visual parent.

## Promotion and approval sequence

After A94, continue only from one exact whole-plush survivor:

1. Complete the untextured constructed sculpt and rerun all views, landmarks,
   topology checks, and two implementation-blind reviews. Stop for the user's
   untextured-sculpt approval.
2. Add fabric/felt materials, pile, stitches, and bounded asymmetry without
   changing approved macro pixels. Stop for material approval.
3. Complete the seated-pose controls, head turn, arm wave, and validation
   animation with clipping/tearing checks. Stop for pose/rig approval.
4. Only after standalone approval, promote the exact asset into the tracked
   `reimu_fumo.blend`, verify LFS/Bazel delivery, and begin Sisyphus or drop
   scene integration.

## Process controls

- Whole-subject pixels, not attempt count, topology, or documentation volume,
  are the unit of progress.
- Keep one stable defect ledger and one fixed comparison packet; do not invent
  new names for recurring helmet/cape/cone failures.
- Beauty review precedes gap counts, IDs, topology inventories, reopen
  matrices, and promotion machinery. Mechanics diagnose a surviving visual
  hypothesis; they do not qualify a failed one.
- Use analytical generation for cameras, measurements, IDs, deterministic
  tests, and simple scaffolds—not as the default author for organic plush
  forms.
- Keep the first-pixel critical path free of rebase, delivery, archival report,
  and optional infrastructure work. Perform those after a decision, then
  commit and push the closed durable attempt.
- Parallelize the two complete visual hypotheses and independent review, not
  several prose variants or mutations of one weak assumption. One coordinator
  chooses the survivor.
- When a branch survives its absolute and comparative gates, promote that
  exact Blend to a deliberate non-temporary Git LFS checkpoint before relying
  on it as a future parent. A digest pointing only into ignored scratch is not
  a remotely durable recovery point.
- Present every user-requested artifact as a direct clickable link.
- Review the complete process again after A94 or immediately after two
  same-category misses, whichever comes first.

## Final stop conditions

Do not silently lower acceptance. Pause for an explicit scope/feasibility
decision only if:

- three genuinely distinct complete macro representations fail the same
  calibrated subject-level gate; or
- the required broad authoring modality is demonstrably unavailable and no
  remaining route can satisfy the requested quality.

Faster rendering, more agent slots, or another report alone is not grounds to
continue or stop.
