## Decision review

**Outcome:** determine quickly whether a jointly edited shallow support,
continuous crown-to-nape yoke, and returning leaf can replace rung 003's deep
helmet without creating a bald region, card/curtain, or detached root.

**Strongest case for the module:** unlike A80's hair-only variants, it can move
the support and visible brown owner together. That is the smallest stated
change that can remove the `0.54--1.02 mm` clearance contradiction while
preserving the anterior face witnesses. A live sparse edit and immediate pixel
gate make the experiment cheap and reversible.

**Strongest recurrence risk:** the connected yoke can remain one uninterrupted
inflated crown/rear mass around a merely shallower support. In profile this
would exchange rung 003's egg for a rounded box, mattress, or mushroom hood,
which is still `D-HEAD-HELMET`. Pulling its lower boundary into four/five lobes
would only decorate that mass; stretching it downward would instead recur as
the full-width curtain/cape form of `D-PANEL-CARD` and `D-REAR-ROOT`. The broad
leaf is also vulnerable to becoming a tube, banana, or isolated paddle.

The decisive falsifier is therefore not "shallower than rung 003." The first
profile/3Q must visibly show all of these at thumbnail scale:

- a compact close crown/rear field with a non-concentric plane/curvature break;
- a separate thin broad returning leaf creating readable overlap and depth;
- continuous brown coverage at the bow root, crown, side, and rear; and
- no single convex arc or vertical wall owning the entire crown-to-nape path.

Front must simultaneously retain one hairline, the reference aperture/fringe
read, and complete brown crown ownership. A shallower helmet, periodic lobe
hem, or leaf-shaped banana is a categorical recurrence, not a parameter issue.

## Verdict

**Proceed conditionally to the first pixel pair.** The joint ownership and live
authoring route are materially different enough to test, and the 12-minute
gate limits cost. Issue an immediate veto on the first recurrence of
`D-HEAD-HELMET`, `D-PANEL-CARD`, or `D-REAR-ROOT`; do not authorize a corrective
Blender cycle. If vetoed, the plan's fresh atomic head-shell maquette is the
credible next family.
