# A81 live joint head-hair rest form

## Decision and dominant defect

A80 closes the hair-only cap/primitive family. The visible cap is only
`0.54--1.02 mm` outside the beige receiver, so preserving coverage preserves
the helmet while meaningful inward hair movement exposes or crosses the
receiver. Another hair-only parameter pass would knowingly repeat the same
structural contradiction.

Test the smallest joint module that can remove it: one shallow beige head
support, one connected brown crown-to-nape yoke, and one broad returning leaf.
Keep the complete plush visible. This is a disposable macro rest-form proof,
not production topology or a promotable asset.

## Changed authoring process

Use the already connected, foreground Blender 5.1.1 Flatpak only as the sole
live mutation owner for an isolated task-owned copy. Edit a small sparse
control mesh directly with references visible and inspect fixed-view pixels
after each bounded change. Do not author another full one-shot geometry
generator, analytic receiver family, sphere, sweep, or disconnected card set.
Every saved live snapshot is untrusted until repository-pinned Blender 5.2.1
clean-reopens it.

Reuse the existing camera/render/comparison harness; the changing payload is
only the joint rest form. Record attempt start, active-worker start,
first-pixel time, and decision time. Target a front plus weakest
profile/three-quarter pair within 12 minutes of active authoring. Review that

Run one implementation-independent standing reviewer concurrently. It sees
the references, rung-003 controls, stable defect ledger, elapsed time, and
pixels, not the author's rationale. The coordinator applies its early-stop

## First observable hypothesis

The shallow support and yoke may share a non-concentric rest form that keeps
the front aperture unchanged while moving visible brown mass from the
upper/deep helmet into a broader lower rear drape. The yoke must continuously
cover the crown and rear, narrow under the bow, and terminate in four or five
unequal soft nape lobes. One thin, broad, two-axis-curved leaf must emerge from
a bow-hidden root, bow outward, cross the rear diagonally, and return toward
the base.

## Pixel gate and stop

Render exact front and the weakest direct profile/three-quarter at 320--512 px
in the unchanged whole-plush context. Reject immediately for any beige bald
patch, duplicate hairline, helmet/egg/rounded box, vertical rear wall,
curtain/card/balloon, tube/banana leaf, detached root, straight or periodic
nape, lost front identity, or regression outside the joint module. A single
veto ends this representation; do not repair it with another Blender cycle.

Only a clean pair earns rear and opposite-side views. Only a full-view survivor
preferred to rung 003 earns topology, material, seam, clean-reopen, rig, or
promotion work. If the joint rest form still cannot escape the helmet, close
the inherited receiver/cap family entirely and begin a fresh atomic head-shell
maquette rather than tuning it.
