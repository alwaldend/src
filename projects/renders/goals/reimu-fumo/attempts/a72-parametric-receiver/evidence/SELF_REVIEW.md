# A72 P0 receiver review

## Verdict

Reset at P0. The deterministic surface is smooth and auditable, but its
separable superellipsoid representation produces exactly the prohibited
rounded-box, mattress, and egg reads. Do not tune its two exponents or derive
fabric panels from it.

## Evidence

- The receiver is one watertight 546-vertex / 576-polygon surface measuring
  about 132 x 99 x 117 mm.
- The five-view clay packet shows squared shoulders and a long, uniform side
  wall in front and three-quarter views.
- The profile is vertically symmetric around a low depth maximum instead of
  placing rear fullness high and turning inward at the nape.
- Single-pole closures visibly pinch the crown and especially the underside.
- Total depth is 99 mm, below the frozen 101.6--112.2 mm band, but increasing
  one depth scalar would not correct the distribution failure.
- Independent implementation-blind review scores receiver macro silhouette
  5/10, below the bounded 6/10 gate, and independently returns RESET.

## Process verdict

The cheap receiver-only gate worked: it rejected the representation before
hair, textures, retopology, rigging, or integration consumed time. The next
attempt must change the shape basis itself. Use independent per-height
half-width, front-reach, and rear-reach profiles plus broad cap rings, so the
controlling silhouettes are direct parameters rather than side effects of two
global exponents.
