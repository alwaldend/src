# A72 P0 receiver-only blind review

## Verdict

**RESET.** The isolated surface is clean and symmetric, but it is not yet a
viable stuffed Fumo receiver. It triggers the attempt's explicit macro veto:
front and three-quarter views read as a rounded box or mattress, while the
profile reads as an egg. Missing hair, face graphics, bow, and body are outside
this review and did not affect the verdict.

**Receiver macro-silhouette score: 5/10.** The broad frontal width and smooth
continuity are useful, but the shape does not reach the required 6/10 viable
rest-surface gate.

## Visible failures

1. A pointed lower-center pole is visible in every view. It reads as a mesh
   closure or nipple rather than a softly turned-in stuffed underside.
2. The side silhouette is an almost vertically symmetric egg. The rear does
   not show a decisive mid/upper-height maximum followed by a lower-rear nape
   undercut.
3. Front and three-quarter silhouettes retain squared superellipse shoulders
   and a long, uniform inflated wall, producing the prohibited rounded-mattress
   read instead of distinct face, crown, gusset, and underside behavior.
4. Rear fullness is centered too low and remains too full toward the base; the
   controlling rear views put the broadest supported mass higher and let the
   lower rear turn inward before the separate hair leaves.
5. The packet's `99 mm` total depth is also below the frozen visible-base band
   of `101.6--112.2 mm`; the pixel failure is distribution, not merely that
   small numeric shortfall.

## Exact next correction

This is not bounded to a scalar tweak of the current power exponents. Replace
the separable vertical/section superellipsoid with an explicit per-height
receiver profile while preserving `Wh = 132 mm` and the isolated scope:

- use independent front, rear, and half-width values at crown, upper quarter,
  mid-height, lower quarter, and underside;
- place maximum rear reach near `0.70` of rear height, then move the lower-rear
  profile forward by `0.05--0.08 Wh` to make the nape undercut visible;
- set base depth near the middle of the allowed band (about `0.80 Wh`,
  `105.6 mm`), without filling the later rear-leaf reserve; and
- replace each single pole closure with several tightening cap rings so crown
  and underside turn in broadly without a center point.

Render that replacement from the same five cameras before adding any hair or
context. If it still reads as a rounded box or egg, stop the analytic receiver
strategy rather than adding panels to conceal it.
