## Review boundary

- Stage: neutral, untextured partial plush sculpt.
- Candidate evidence: S07 fixed-camera `front`, `three_quarter`, and
  `three_quarter_mirror` renders.
- Comparison evidence: the same S03 views, reviewed only after the S07 absolute
  pass.
- Primary references: `canonical_front_25cm.png` for frontal hair coverage and
  `canonical_turn_180.gif` for crown depth, side/rear silhouette, and layer
  order.
- Supporting references: `physical_front.png`, `physical_side.png`,
  `clean_front.png`, `turn.gif`, and `sofa.gif` for fabric construction,
  softness, and continuity.
- Explicit module scope: crown behind the preserved front fringe and cheek
  locks. Lower rear hair and bow remain absent and are not scored as missing
  parts.
- Review limitation: no direct side or rear S07 render is present, so this is
  a provisional module decision rather than a multi-view sculpt approval.
- Context limitation: the reviewer was told the intended edit in the task
  brief. That description was not treated as evidence; the verdict below is
  based on rendered pixels.

## Absolute S07 review

S07 reads as one continuous shallow hair shell seated behind a separate soft
front layer. The crown is closed in all supplied views. The front preserves
brown coverage to both temples without the white gaps seen in S04. Both
obliques show an uninterrupted crown surface and a lower rear boundary that
now has enough length and curvature to plausibly receive overlapping lower
hair panels.

The module is still deliberately incomplete. Its exposed rear/lower boundary
looks broad and plain in isolation, and the smooth crown remains at some risk
of reading as a cap until the lower rear leaves, bow roots, seam tension, and
later fabric treatment provide construction context. Those are not reasons to
reject this bounded crown foundation: there is no visible hole, clipping,
floating temple, or categorical wrong-medium failure in S07 itself.

The named S03 failures visibly improve, and the protected frontal silhouette
does not regress. S07 therefore wins the keep-or-undo comparison.

## Decision

**Preserve S07 as the provisional crown-module survivor.** It is safe to use
as the frozen input to the next bounded module. It is not a passed standalone
sculpt, not a material-stage authorization, and not evidence that the full
hair system is complete.

Do not continue reshaping the crown merely to make this partial render look
finished. The next geometric owner should be the lower-rear hair panel system.
Its interface contract is:

1. overlap or tuck beneath S07's lower rear edge without exposing the head;
2. break the current broad cap silhouette into the layered, softly stuffed
   leaves visible in the canonical turn;
3. preserve S07's closed top, front height, and bilateral temple coverage; and
4. verify the completed contact from direct side and rear cameras before the
   combined hair subsystem can become a checkpoint.

No bounded correction to S07 itself is required before that integration test.
