## Decision and bounded hypothesis

A one-piece extracted head shell is the simplest geometry but is rejected by
the repeated helmet evidence. A complete multi-part hairstyle is too large an
uncertain chunk. The smallest credible next module is two shallow constructed
crown fabric panels, left and right, meeting at a visible but soft center seam
and overlapping behind the A87 fringe root. This corresponds to sewn plush
construction and leaves a defined rear edge for later rear/lower hair panels.
