# A88 result — preserve the S07 two-panel crown module

A88 closes as **refine** with S07 as the exact provisional crown survivor. It
is not the standalone untextured sculpt and does not satisfy full-goal
approval. It is the next immutable parent for a separate lower-rear hair
module.

The freehand sparse grids in S00 and S01 failed immediately: they intersected
the receiver, opened holes, and read as paired inflated buns. S02 reset the
implementation to a level-1 contact template derived from the frozen S12 cage,
split into two non-overlapping panel owners. S03 added controlled subdivision
and established the first soft, continuous crown volume, but retained an
exposed head slit at the apex and a dead horizontal rear receiver edge. S04
and S05 incorrectly treated that slit as a seam/coverage problem. S06 proved
the causal issue was the directly flattened panel sitting outside its cloth
clearance. S07 changes only the outward felt/pile clearance to `2.2 mm`, closes
the exposed receiver in beauty and ID pixels, preserves the A87 front frame,
and retains the curved rear overlap edge for the next owner.

Independent blind review preserves S07 and explicitly rejects more isolated
crown work. The six-view packet confirms a continuous upper crown in both
profiles and a bounded rear rim. The still-visible lower rear and side receiver
is not a hidden failure or crown responsibility: the next attempt must tuck
separate lower-rear hair panels beneath this rim and break the cap silhouette
into the soft staggered leaves visible in the physical and turn references.

## Process audit

The module decomposition worked: A87 remained frozen throughout eight A88
states, and the failure was isolated to crown contact. The main inefficiency
was three incorrect diagnoses of the same bright apex slit. Beauty pixels alone
did not distinguish a seam gap, missing coverage, and receiver breakthrough;
the component-ID pass plus single-variable thickness test did. For future
interfaces, inspect owner-ID pixels before changing topology, and test physical
clearance before extending coverage. Continue to render front/three-quarter
first, but promote to direct profile/rear evidence as soon as a crown or rear
interface passes the initial packet.
