## Decision review

A domain expert could reject another head-focused attempt because the whole
model also has an oversized bow, conical lower stack, stiff sleeves, and poor
plush construction. That objection is valid if this becomes a detail cycle.
However, exact side and three-quarter evidence identifies the deep monolithic
head/hair receiver as a dominant whole-subject proportion error, and A68/A69
have now falsified both local sculpting and deformation of its current
representation. A whole-model rebuild would broaden uncertainty and erase
verified face identity without first proving a viable head construction.

Verdict: replace the smallest structurally wrong receiver/cap module in an
isolated copy, but treat the whole five-view subject as the acceptance context.
Stop at the first coarse render if the new module does not materially improve
whole-subject silhouette and plush read. Do not transfer locks, seams,
materials, or detail until that gate passes.
