Reset. A70 replaced the old helmet pair in an isolated live copy with a closed
gusseted receiver and separate closed hair panels, but the representation
failed the first fixed-view clay gate. It must not continue to C1, materials,
attachment transfer, or detail. The rung-003 parent and tracked reusable asset
remain unchanged.

## Work performed

- Established independent reference and topology contracts using every
  controlling front, profile, rear, three-quarter, and physical reference.
- Corrected the previous assumption that the problem was total depth: the old
  receiver was already inside the broad depth band. The actual target is depth
  distribution and visibly separate layer ownership.
- Built a 482-vertex closed front/gusset/rear receiver and two 234-vertex
  closed crown/rear hair yokes in the user-requested Blender 5.1.1 Flatpak
  live host.
- Saved four immutable live snapshots and reopened/rendered each with pinned

  panels, rounded front panels, and broad side coverage.
- Stopped at the C0 veto after broad side coverage became a rigid card/bridge
  and recreated the filled helmet envelope.

## Strategy reset

Independent extruded silhouette panels do not share a curved rest surface, so
adding coverage creates masks, cards, or continuous bridges. The next attempt
must first establish one coherent sculpted macro volume against the fixed
multi-view silhouettes, then derive constructed panels and thickness from that
approved surface. It must not reuse the old helmet and must not retopologize or
detail before the clay-only macro volume passes.

## Whole-process audit

The Flatpak live loop is useful: geometry changes take seconds and pinned
five-view packets roughly 33 seconds. The process mistake was spending three
refinements on panel coverage after the first packet exposed the absent shared
surface. Future cycles will render one silhouette-owning macro volume before
secondary panels. One coordinator remains the sole `.blend` writer; parallel
agents remain useful for independent contracts and pixel review.
