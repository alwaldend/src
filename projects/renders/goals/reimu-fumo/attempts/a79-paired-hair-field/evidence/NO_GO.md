## Decisive representation failures

1. Private per-triangle centroid padding over ear-clipped pockets would create
   triangular dimpling and faceting rather than one shared sewn panel field.
2. Eight front-root controls are too sparse relative to the protected cap's
   contiguous 196-vertex aperture. Interpolating 64 samples across those eight
   controls could chord across receiver curvature and falsely satisfy a
   coverage percentage.
3. The deliberately near-planar rear closed pocket remains a thin shield/card;
   naming outer and contact skins does not supply the needed transverse and
   longitudinal fabric camber or rolled edge behavior.
4. The proposed rear-coverage mask could reward an oversized planar occluder.
   It did not yet enforce the canonical rear width-by-height curve, narrow
   under-bow start, maximum-width height, unequal lower lobes, or diagonal
   layer-order bounds.

These are representation-level recurrences of A74's canopy/card family and
A77's card-prone rear leaf. They are not eligible for a bounded Blender fix,
more topology, materials, or rendering.
