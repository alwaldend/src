## Explicit uncertainty

No controlling tank, water appearance, camera composition, fluid behavior, or
storyboard reference was supplied for this successor module. Tank dimensions,
lighting, and framing are therefore neutral engineering choices, not visual
fidelity claims. The first water contact uses the elementary vacuum-fall
relation `t = sqrt(2 h / g)` only to make the proxy timing falsifiable. It does
not model air drag, fabric deformation, entrained air, buoyancy, absorption,
or post-impact motion.
