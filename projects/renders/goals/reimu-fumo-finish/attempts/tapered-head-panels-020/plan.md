# Tapered stuffed panels and collar seating

## Decision

Proceed with a reconstructed depth profile, not another uniform head scale.
The independent reference review confirms that the prior 0.747 Wh maximum
depth was plausible but distributed almost constantly around the perimeter.
The side cylinder and lower-head clearance falsified attempt 019.

The strongest counterargument is that a smooth lens alone could still read as
a helmet. Preserve a broad facial plane and the useful traced fringe contour,
but make the crown and underside substantially shallower than the mid-head.
Use explicit front and rear panel depth fields meeting at one soft perimeter,
without a long constant-depth annular wall. Seat the whole head/fringe/face
layout 6 mm lower against the unchanged collar. The crown-to-chin height stays
near 0.986 Wh. This tests depth distribution and contact, not material polish.

Keep head width 0.1165 m and maximum depth inside the frozen 0.66-0.82 Wh
band. Measure depth at crown-relative heights 0.15, 0.50, and 0.85; their
relative narrowing is a construction witness, not a new reference-derived
numeric acceptance threshold. Front collar separation must be under 0.02 Wh,
with no new floating bow root, visible gap, hard shell arc, or card-like lock.
