contour support and thickness-aware seating. They do not establish attractive
hair. 053's radial curtain, 054's covering/cream gap, and 055's segmented sewn
assembly are rejected. Do not resume any of these saved meshes as accepted,
hide their defects with fibers, or repeat another radius/hem-dose variant.

The next design must replace the shared head/fringe/lock joins with a directly
editable low-resolution construction cage whose seam paths are designed
together. Prove a continuous cap-to-fringe face and non-horizontal side/nape
boundary in neutral front/side/oblique views before multiplying mesh density.
Maintain the canonical measurements and actual support checks. This is a next
strategy requirement, not a prepared or executed new model.
