## Bounded plane dose response

Clean-open the exact partial three-stroke state. Add identical three-stroke
blocks, measuring after every block at cumulative totals 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21,
24, and at most 27 strokes. Stop immediately once independently recomputable

Abort the coupon on any of these conditions:

- non-plane or `CONTROL_plane` displacement above `1e-6`;
- cumulative maximum displacement above `0.20` scene units;
- plane variance increases from one completed block to the next;
- two consecutive blocks each reduce absolute variance by less than
