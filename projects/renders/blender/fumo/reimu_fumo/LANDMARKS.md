# Reimu Fumo reference landmarks

## Canonical exact-variant authority

The durable `references/canonical_front_25cm.png` and
`references/canonical_turn_180.gif` now supersede this document's original
physical-front registration for exact-variant identity. The canonical front
controls the 25 cm scale, front proportions, graphics, and silhouette; the
canonical turn controls depth, layer order, and side/rear silhouettes. Their
active head datum is `Wh = 368 ± 4 px`, center `x = 485`, crown top
`y = 231`, central fringe tip `(.588,.677)`, and visible-beige exposure
`.603 ± .03 Wh` in both axes. That beige value is a visible-pixel mask,
not the inferred full or hidden opening. The `1.098 Wh` crown-to-lowest-lock
measurement includes the separate locks; a lock-excluded crown/head witness
ends near `.986 ± .03 Wh`. Reserve `.35 ± .05 Wh` behind the shallow
base head for the independent rear hair layer.

Canonical front garment/accessory checks use the same `Wh` datum: bow span
`2.038 Wh`, combined sleeve span `1.277 Wh`, red skirt width `0.916 Wh`,
ruffled hem width `1.065 Wh`, individual visible foot widths
`0.212–0.217 Wh`, foot-pair outer span `0.774 Wh`, and foot-center separation
`0.560 Wh`. These exact-variant values control direct conflicts with the
supporting physical-front measurements below.

The `Wh = 189 px`, `1.03 Wh`, opening `.78 × .60 Wh`, and fringe tip
`(.49,.57)` values below remain a supporting physical-front-variant record.
They may control fabric/construction checks for that source, but they must not
override or be averaged into the canonical exact-variant registration.

This contract was frozen before the canonical exact-variant sources were
designated. Subject to the supersession above, it remains useful supporting
evidence. Reference variation is represented through rig or shape controls
rather than averaged into a new plush design.

## Supporting physical-front coordinates and alignment

`Wh = 1.000` is the outer front head/hair envelope width, excluding the bow and
red side ribbon tabs. The primary physical-front source measures `Wh = 189 px`
from `(143, 104)` to `(332, 298)`. Image coordinates begin at the top-left.
The matching `1.03 Wh` height below also belongs to that coupled outer
head/hair envelope; it is not the height of the mostly occluded beige support
cushion.

For front comparison, scale the candidate projection until the outer head/hair
width equals `Wh`, align the head envelope's horizontal center, and align its
top. Position and size errors are measured in `Wh`; no later candidate may
change this alignment. Each controlled front landmark must differ by at most
`0.05 Wh`. Depth measurements use their stated frozen bands because the only
high-resolution side source is oblique and partially occluded.

The asset faces `-Y`, uses `Z` up, and contacts the ground at `Z = 0`. Fixed
measurement cameras are square orthographic views with scale `2.36 Wh` and
target height `1.055 Wh`:

- front at `(0, -8, 1.055)`;
- side at `(8, 0, 1.055)`;
- rear at `(0, 8, 1.055)`; and
- three-quarter at `(5.657, -5.657, 1.055)`.

The camera distances may be multiplied uniformly without changing projection.
Resolution is 640 by 640 for review. A separate perspective presentation camera
does not supply measurements.

## Supporting source authority

- The physical front supports stuffing, garment construction, cloth behavior,
  and uncertainty checks; it does not select the canonical bow or feet.
- The clean front supports eye, mouth, fringe, and other graphic landmarks
  where it does not conflict with the canonical exact variant.
- The older turn GIF supports otherwise missing continuity. The canonical
  30-frame turn controls exact-variant side/rear silhouette and layer order.
- The physical side/rear three-quarter supports depth order, panel thickness,
  skirt footprint, and foot construction, but not exact orthographic
  dimensions.
- The sofa GIF controls seated compression, fabric pile, and close-up panel
  behavior only.

There is no high-resolution true rear image. Rear judgments use the complete
turn GIF with lower measurement confidence and must not claim front-view
precision.

## Identity contour

The physical-front hairline follows these approximate pixels:

`(180, 198) → (203, 160) → (235, 211) → (259, 164) → (285, 199) → (302, 181)`.

The central fringe point is broad and low, not a thin spike. Side locks are
about `0.16 Wh` wide and terminate at the lower head envelope. The complete
eye applique includes the beige upper section, straight dark lid, rounded red
lower section, and small highlight; it is not a raised pill.

## Frozen exclusions and variant handling

- Do not derive true depth from either front still.
- Do not derive orthographic depth directly from the physical oblique side.
- Do not derive overall height or unoccluded foot size from the panning,
  bottom-clipped sofa GIF.
- Do not use low-resolution GIF pixels for fine texture or sub-`0.02 Wh`
  boundaries.
- The clean-front bow span (`1.94 Wh`), older turn-GIF span (`1.37 Wh`), and
  physical-front span (`1.69 Wh`) are supporting alternate cloth states. The
  exact canonical front controls the base `2.038 Wh` state; future controls
  may reach supported alternates without changing the base construction.
- Supporting physical-reference foot widths range from `0.25–0.32 Wh`. The
  exact canonical front controls the base visible widths
  `0.212–0.217 Wh`; the physical-front `0.30 Wh` value is not the default.
