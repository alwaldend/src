# Curved cap representation cycle

## Representation change

Return to protected A157. Retain its soft head cushion, separate A44 fringe
panels, A45 cheek/ribbon panels, eyes, mouth, and three rear locks. Hide only
the continuous helmet cap.

Replace it with a thin superellipsoid-derived surface that covers the crown,
rear, and upper sides but has a generous front opening and no lower-side slab.
Give the surface fabric-scale thickness and a softened sewn boundary. Move the
two cheek panels outward only enough to restore the canonical visible face
width.

## Expected result and gate

The front should retain A157's identity layout while exposing a wider, flatter
face plane. The side should show a shallow cushion, a thin crown/rear cap, and
overlapping cheek/rear locks instead of a deep continuous helmet or rectangle.

Pinned front and side renders are the first gate. Reset without correction if
the head still reads as a helmet/card, if the cap creates an open jagged edge,
if the depth bands fail, or if bow/body landmarks move by more than `0.05 Wh`.
Only a passing pair may advance to measurement and five-view review.
