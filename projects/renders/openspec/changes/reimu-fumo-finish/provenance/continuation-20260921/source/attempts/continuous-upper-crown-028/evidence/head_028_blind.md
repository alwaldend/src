## Relative to 027c: retain as an intermediate

forehead wall and abrupt top corner have become a broad curved profile.
The upper front transition in both three-quarter views also reads softer.
Front identity and temple coverage are preserved. Rear and lower-body forms
show no material change at this image scale.

No major new regression is visible. The forehead bulge is now more prominent
in side view, but within these references it is preferable to the previous
flat wall. The apex and side ridge remain unresolved rather than newly
introduced failures. This is a genuine intermediate shape improvement, not
just a cosmetic material change, but it does not pass absolute construction.

Recommendation: retain 028 over 027c. Continue at head construction and macro
proportion level. A broad continuous crown/front/side transition, fuller hair
framing, and reference-matched head width are higher priority than surface
detail. Do not interpret intermediate retention as approval of the head or
the complete plush.
