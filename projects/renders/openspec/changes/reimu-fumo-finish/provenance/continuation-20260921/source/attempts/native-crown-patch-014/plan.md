## Diagnosed mechanism and causal change

`A44 continuous hair cap with smooth opening` is one connected 9,449-vertex
surface covering 81.4 percent of the head at an almost uniform 0.72 to 0.82 mm
offset. The resulting second skin, rather than excessive total depth, creates
the dominant helmet read. The underlying head and outer-hair depth already sit
inside their frozen bands.

Earlier plans generated replacement boxes, shells, or radial pillows. This
plan reopens head work only because `macro-sculpt-010` proved stable native
interactive edit control. It reuses already fitted topology but removes the
enclosing region instead of generating or tuning another full-head shell.
