# Shingled padded-lock hair plan

## Hypothesis

Four independently closed curved crown cushions, two independently closed side
locks, and the retained lower fringe/cheek/rear locks can cover the beige head
substrate without any one hair object enclosing the head. Quiet overlap at the
crown and negative space only among lower tips should read as sewn hair panels
rather than a helmet, card box, or radial wig.

## Bounded change

1. Reopen protected A157 as layout donor.
2. Hide the continuous cap and both triangular temple-transition panels.
3. Keep the beige stuffed head, three front fringe panels, two A45 cheek locks,
   and three A42 rear locks.
4. Add four explicit closed, curved, padded crown meshes sampled around the
   head surface. Each has independent inner and outer grids, bridged rounded
   edges, quiet overlap, smooth shading, and subdivision.
5. Add one broad tapered closed side-lock cushion per side to bridge crown,
   cheek, and rear layers without spanning the head.
6. Preserve face, bow, costume, body, and cameras unchanged.

No plane-plus-Solidify panel, curve rail, cube, full-head shell, rigging,
material polish, or downstream integration is authorized.

## Stop gate

Clean-reopen the first state in pinned Blender and render front, side, and the
more revealing three-quarter view. Reset without correction if any view shows
an exposed pale crown, radial flower/scallop, helmet band, rectangular wall,
flat card, seam reaching the silhouette, floating root, gap above `0.0028 m`,
or crossing above `0.0028 m`.

Only if all three pass may the rear and mirrored three-quarter views be
rendered and measured. The complete whole-character clay stage still cannot
pass while bow, sleeve, skirt/contact, or foot macro failures remain.
