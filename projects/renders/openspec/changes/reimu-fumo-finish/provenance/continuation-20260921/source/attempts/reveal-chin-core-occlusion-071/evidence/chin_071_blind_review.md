Five largest visible discrepancies, in order:

1. The head/hair mass has a continuous, smoothly inflated dome and deep rounded rear profile. Physical side and turntable references show a more evidently constructed cushion and layered fabric hair. This is the principal medium/construction failure.
2. Sleeve openings form regular thick tubes with smoothly rounded inner hands. Reference openings collapse and fold around their contents, giving a softer, less rigid construction.
3. The lower face rolls inward sharply toward the collar in side view. The transition reads slightly tucked and compressed rather than a broad stuffed underside resting on clothing.
4. Bow tails and upper wings retain unusually regular panel outlines and abrupt plane changes compared with the physical references' folded, sagging fabric. Missing fibers are not the issue.
5. Feet present as straight short cylinders with nearly flat dark ends; photographed feet have softer pod silhouettes and less regular transitions into the garment.

## Bounded lower-face/chin/collar finding

Likeness **7/10**; cushion construction **7/10**; visible contact/occlusion **7.5/10**.

Front seating is reasonably convincing: the chin sits immediately above the collar. Side view exposes the pronounced retreat of the lower face, while three-quarter shows a narrow dark underside band above the collar. Together these create a slightly tucked-back chin and shallow visual step. The references support close head-to-collar seating but a softer, broader stuffed transition.

There is **no clearly visible through-background neck gap**, and these pixels do not prove floating, penetration, or any hidden geometry defect. The dark band could include contact shadow and hair occlusion. The objection is the visible contour and abruptness of the seating, not an inferred mesh gap.

Suggested next construction direction: establish a broader flattened cushion underside meeting the collar, checked against the physical side/turntable views. Preserve compact seating; merely exposing more neck would not address the observed form. Rear and uncropped presentation evidence remain necessary for any full gate. Root owns final acceptance.
