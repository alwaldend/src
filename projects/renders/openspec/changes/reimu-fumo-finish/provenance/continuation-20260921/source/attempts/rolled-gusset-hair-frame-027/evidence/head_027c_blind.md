Largest discrepancies:

1. Side head profile has a straight front wall and pronounced upper corner,
   followed by a visible ridge into the rounded rear mass. It reads as a
   separate shaped mask rather than the reference's softly continuous sewn
   cushion. The crown join is much less objectionable than 027b, but the
   volume transition remains a construction problem.
2. Head/face is narrow relative to the broad bow and garment. Reference hair
   framing and side locks are fuller, softer, and more integrated; these locks
   hang as slender straight panels.
3. Bow wings are very wide and shallow in front and thin in side. Reference
   loops show greater vertical fullness and soft fabric gathering.
4. Dress remains a flared smooth cone with regular triangular lower trim,
   rather than the compact gathered skirt and irregular ruffle in the photo.
5. Open tubular sleeve mouths lack the reference's pale stuffed hands, and
   the feet lack the reference's visible pale stuffed sections.

## Relative to hem_026: modest progress, retain as an intermediate

027c improves the large exposed angular temple regions visible on 026 in
both three-quarter views. Hair framing looks more deliberate there, and the
forehead is less uniformly spherical. Those are visible construction and
volume changes, not merely surface finishing. Front eye-opening peaks still
have small jagged steps; they are not fully resolved.

The tradeoff is the new angular upper forehead profile in side view. The
baseline had a smoother but more spherical outline. I classify this as a
visible moderate regression, not a new catastrophic discontinuity: 027c has
no exposed pale crown strip and no obviously floating front slab. The side
profile still fails absolute fidelity, so retention must not imply that head
construction has passed. Rear and lower-body forms appear unchanged.

Recommendation: retain 027c as a modest intermediate improvement over 026,
with the side crown/front transition explicitly unresolved. Do not advance
to material or microdetail work. The next head result must replace the upper
corner and front-wall impression with a continuous softly compressed cushion
profile while preserving the cleaner three-quarter temple coverage. This is
worth keeping as a working direction, not worth presenting as a finished head.
