# Central underside: reference direction

Image-only assessment, 2026-09-05. Revisited canonical_front_25cm.png and
physical_side.png specifically for underside ordering; compared with already
viewed retained 033. No geometry or implementation inspected. Optional turn
frames 11/16 were not found in the bounded reference-folder filename query
and were not reviewed. This note adds no acceptance thresholds.

Direct front-reference observation: the red skirt ends in a white gathered
ruffle. Beneath it, two pale stuffed sections lead forward/down into two
separate black rounded toes. Between those pale sections, background is
visible below the central ruffle, forming a tapered opening toward the hem.
The central ruffle partly covers the roots. There is no conspicuous broad
horizontal black strip joining the feet in this view.

Direct physical-side observation: a pale stuffed section occupies the span
from beneath the hem to the nearer black toe. The cloth hem lies above and
partly over it. Dark shading occurs at contacts, but the visible lower body
does not present as a wide black platform. This angle does not expose the
central gap well enough to determine its hidden construction.

Visual direction supported by those observations: maintain distinct black
toe ends, visible pale stuffed sections behind them, and an irregular cloth
hem above/over their roots. In front, the space below the middle ruffle and
between the inner leg edges should mostly read as background plus narrow
contact shadow, rather than a continuous dark horizontal mass. The observed
033 band instead links the feet visually and makes the skirt read as resting
on a rigid dark base.

Uncertainty: the references do not establish the color or shape of every
hidden underside surface, nor prove that no connecting stuffed body exists
behind the hem. The front opening supports a projected visibility result,
not an instruction to remove all central body structure. Contact shadows and
small occluded areas can be dark; what lacks reference support is their
current broad, straight, dominant presentation. This note makes no modeling
recipe or claim about the source of the rendered band.
