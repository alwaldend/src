## Absolute whole-image review

Unlabeled recognition: **yes**, the same Reimu Fumo subject is readily recognizable. Exact photographed variant fidelity is not fully established.

Intended medium: recognizably a plush-styled character, but its visible construction is still too uniformly molded in several large components for an approval-stage sewn-plush match. This judgment concerns macro form and attachment, not the absence of final fibers or material work.

Largest visible discrepancies, ordered by impact:

1. **Head/hair construction — major.** The crown and side/back hair form a strongly continuous inflated shell with smooth rounded edges. The three-quarter/isolated views emphasize its bulbous uninterrupted volume. Physical/turning references have more evident shallow panel structure, seam tension, and draping changes between cap and hanging sections. The front graphic outline is recognizable, but the full volume still has a helmet-like read.
2. **Sleeve construction — major local mismatch.** Side and three-quarter views show a thick, regular, rounded white tunnel around the arm. The physical-side and turning references show thinner flexible fabric walls, a sagging opening, and uneven compression around the stuffed hand. The current regular rim and cavity dominate the side-view clothing read.
3. **Bow construction — moderate.** Large wing borders and surfaces are unusually smooth and regular. The physical references show stronger gathering near the knot and irregular edge folds. Overall red/white bow identity and span remain readable.
4. **Side-lock construction — moderate.** Tied locks present as thick, smoothly pointed paddles with limited bending or seam compression, especially in the close-up. Physical references show flatter, softer pieces that change direction at the ties and roots. This is a fabric-construction concern, not proof that their roots float.
5. **Lower-face variant mismatch — minor/uncertain.** The central chin edge is straighter than the canonical product reference, with a relatively abrupt corner near the right lock in the close-up. The physical-front reference substantially reduces the severity of this discrepancy. It does not justify labeling ordinary underside rounding as recession.
