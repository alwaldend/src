# Integrated textile hair plan

## Hypothesis

The existing soft head cushion can serve as the sewn hair substrate when its
surface is brown outside a controlled front face opening. Existing independent
front fringe, cheek-lock, and rear-lock cushions then provide silhouette and
layering. This changes the representation from a second head-covering shell to
one stuffed substrate with textile regions and independently rooted locks.

helmet rim, planar side card, pale uncovered crown, hard opening rail, broken
fringe, or disconnected head layer.
