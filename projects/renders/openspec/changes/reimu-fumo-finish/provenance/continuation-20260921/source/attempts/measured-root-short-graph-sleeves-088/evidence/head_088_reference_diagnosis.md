The front proportions already approach the canonical envelope. The dominant
problem is how the head and hair meet and turn in depth. Uniformly enlarging
the head or extending the chin would not address the strongest visible
evidence. This is a head-only construction diagnosis, not an asset acceptance
review; no camera-calibrated measurements or overlay pass is claimed.

1. **Lower-face roll is more consequential than chin height.** In
   [front](chin_087_all_review/front.png), the lower face finishes in a fairly
   level base with abrupt lower-cheek corners. The
   [canonical front](../../../projects/renders/assets/reimu_fumo/references/canonical_front_25cm.png)
   has a more continuous broad U from cheek to cheek. In the
   [isolated three-quarter](chin_087_close_review/isolated_three_quarter.png),
   the large facial field rolls abruptly into a dark narrow underside and
   basal lip; the bottom reads tucked or pinched despite the substantial
   surrounding head mass. This is a surface-transition observation, not proof
   of a particular mesh defect. Build the front as a softly filled cushion
   panel whose lower cheeks turn continuously into an underside with readable
   thickness. Preserve the useful front landmarks while redistributing the
   roll across the lower face and rim. A local protruding chin or an inflated
   whole head risks a muzzle or balloon. The
   [side](chin_087_all_review/side.png) already shows substantial depth; the
   heavily occluded [reference side](side_062_frame_12.png) does not establish
   an exact missing chin depth.

2. **The face opening reads as a graphic cutout in a helmet-shaped mass.**
   The isolated view exposes the same broad curvature across brown crown and
   pale face, with a very clean scalloped boundary and a pale lateral lobe
   below the fringe. The [presentation](chin_087_all_review/presentation.png)
   makes that opening feel inset beneath a smooth cap. Compare the overlapping
   fabric-edge reading around the cheeks and bangs in the canonical front and
   [oblique reference](side_062_frame_10.png). Hair occlusion can therefore
   make the face seem recessed even if the overall head outline is adequate.
   Construct a continuous soft face cushion and a conforming hair cover with
   a thin, readable fringe edge; let the cover establish the opening through
   overlap and edge tension. This is a visible construction direction, not a
   requirement for separate objects or a claim about the present topology.
   Avoid individually inflated bang segments: they would preserve the helmet
   problem in smaller pieces.

3. **Side and rear locks interrupt the cloth continuity.** In both
   [three-quarter](chin_087_all_review/three_quarter.png) and
   [mirrored three-quarter](chin_087_all_review/three_quarter_mirror.png),
   the tied front locks read as tapered blades while narrow rear strips hang
   from visibly segmented side surfaces. The [rear](chin_087_all_review/rear.png)
   reinforces the abrupt panel divisions beside the smooth central mass.
   Reference frames 10 and 12 support broader, softly tapering cloth masses
   that wrap the side of the head, with compressed tie regions and deliberate
   overlaps. Rebuild the visible side-hair flow as broad fabric panels seated
   into the cover, then define only the supported tapered ends and tie
   compression. Do not preserve free strips merely because their front tips
   land correctly. The bow hides much of the reference rear construction;
   these images cannot prescribe its exact unseen seams or lock count.

Recommended next construction trial: establish the cushion front/rim and
conforming hair cover together, retaining front graphic landmarks; seat the
side panels to that receiving surface. Judge the isolated head and assembled
head at the same fixed front, side, and both three-quarter views. The trial
must remove the pinched basal-lip reading, maintain the rounded frontal chin
arc, and replace blade/helmet transitions with continuous soft-panel overlap.
Those are distinct checks: passing the front outline alone cannot establish
surface fullness or correct hair occlusion. Apply the existing measured
tolerances after camera alignment; the current images alone do not justify
new geometric amplitudes. The reference-fidelity skill's stalled-subsystem
reset and plush construction guidance support this representation trial.
