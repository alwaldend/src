Five largest discrepancies, ordered by impact:

1. **Hair/head construction — shape evidence.** Side and three-quarter renders show a continuous, smoothly rounded cap with uniformly full hanging locks and blunt rear ends. The physical references show thinner hanging fabric pieces, irregular bends, and more distinct panel transitions. The candidate's face-to-hair boundary reads as a rigid shell opening. Existing side depth is substantial: these pixels do **not** justify globally inflating the head or hair.
2. **Surface response — material/lighting inference.** Hair and face have uninterrupted smooth gradients; bow tails have broad polished-looking highlights. The physical photographs visibly separate short-pile hair, matte face cloth, embroidered eyes, and woven red fabric. At this render scale the candidate offers little comparable separation. This supports a synthetic appearance, not a claim about the underlying shader or absence of microscopic fibers.
3. **Sleeves — shape evidence.** Side and three-quarter openings have thick, rounded, regular rims that resemble rigid tubes. The photographed sleeves have thin, uneven folded edges and sagging panel openings. More inflation would strengthen the problem; thin edges, panel drape, and localized fold/compression are the supported direction.
4. **Skirt and feet — shape evidence.** The lower edge becomes a stack of irregular frills and exposed underlayers, especially from the side. References show a more legible continuous garment edge and gathered trim. The candidate feet have exceptionally regular circular ends and straight cylindrical profiles; soften their contour/contact locally without inventing anatomy or extra volume.
5. **Face and bow detail — mixed evidence.** The tiny mouth is almost lost, eyes look like clean graphic inserts, and the bow's white border reads as thick piping/scalloping rather than the photographed narrow pleated trim. The bow can appropriately remain thin; its edge construction matters more than bulk.

No rear render, calibrated overlay, neutral sculpt, or full-resolution material close-up was supplied. These scores assess visible appearance, not measured tolerance, hidden attachment integrity, or export readiness.
