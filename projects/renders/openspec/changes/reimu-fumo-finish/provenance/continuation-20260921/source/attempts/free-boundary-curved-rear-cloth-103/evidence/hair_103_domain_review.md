## Why the suspected core gate is stronger, but harmless here

The inherited core loop unconditionally queries every rear-core vertex before
running the complete PL overlap majorant. That is a stronger premise than
clearance against cloth wherever cloth actually exists: an absent receiver
does not itself invalidate an overlap-based correction. Nevertheless, the
measured frozen chart passes that premise, so no clarification or gate removal
is required for this attempt.

If a later domain change creates misses, it must not invent an extrapolated
receiver or drop an entire core face merely because its vertex rays miss.
A triangle can intersect the receiving chart even when all three vertices lie
outside it. The full triangle overlay still finds that overlap, and assigning
the complete overlap bound to all three subject vertices makes their affine
correction a majorant throughout it. Y-only correction leaves the projected
domain unchanged. This mathematical distinction was independently challenged
and confirmed; it supplies no authority to revise this frozen plan.

Own-XZ liner queries are different: their graph values construct the liner
coordinates. A missing query would leave that construction undefined and
cannot simply be reported-and-skipped. Both query stages pass here.
