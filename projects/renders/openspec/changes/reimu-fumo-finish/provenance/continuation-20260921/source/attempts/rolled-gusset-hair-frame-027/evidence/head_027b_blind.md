## Relative verdict: reject as a retained checkpoint

There is a real but limited directional improvement: the front eye openings
are cleaner triangles, and the profile is less spherical at the forehead.
That improvement is visible without knowing how the model was made.

However, the candidate trades the baseline's continuous rounded crown for a
separated plate, exposed pale crown seam, and harsher side discontinuities.
These are large construction failures in the side and both three-quarter
views, not local finishing defects. Baseline has imperfect jagged lower hair
edges too, but its crown is materially more coherent. Rear and garment
silhouettes show no meaningful difference at this image scale.

Recommendation: do not retain head_027b as net visual progress. Preserve the
idea of a less spherical forehead only as an experiment. Rebuild the head's
front, crown, and side transition as one visually continuous soft cushion
construction before refining the fringe. A clean unified silhouette in side
and three-quarter views is the next necessary result; smoothing the exposed
edges alone would not establish reference fidelity.
