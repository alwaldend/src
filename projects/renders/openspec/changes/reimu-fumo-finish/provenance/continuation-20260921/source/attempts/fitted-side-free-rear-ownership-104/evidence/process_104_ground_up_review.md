## Stable process defects and concrete changes

1. FUMO-MACRO-FOUNDATION: treating101 as a geometrically sound foundation.
   Evidence tier: live pinned renders, implementation-blind103image review,
   and root/independent current visual critique. Keep101recoverable; build one
   fresh low-complexity whole-asset study from references, not a repair of101.
   Judge the whole shape before deciding which old components merit reuse.

2. FUMO-FEEDBACK-INVERSION: high-cost final-construction constraints before
   cheap visual proof.104's new paired clipper and chained builder reached a
   liner failure before any useful appearance decision. Save clearly labeled
   concept drafts and render early. Basic file/finite-geometry safety remains;
   temporary simplifications/open cloth or intersections are disclosed as
   draft defects, never production acceptance. Full technical checks still
   apply before construction-stage retention and final delivery.

3. FUMO-REFERENCE-CALIBRATION: repeated reference-informed formulas have not
   produced the required calibrated likeness evidence. Reuse the actual
   reference dossier/landmarks, but resolve one controlling source per target,
   match front camera and disclose side/rear uncertainty before geometry work.
   Do not average canonical and physical variant differences. Compare aligned
   silhouettes and component relationships, not an attractive isolated render.

4. FUMO-PROCEDURAL-LOCKIN: dense inherited triangles and exact-coordinate
   preservation make broad changes unnecessarily hard. Use a small editable
   cage/panel representation for the study. Scripts remain useful for scene
   setup, repeatable cameras, saving and checks; they should not substitute
   for direct visual control of cushion shape and cloth outline. Direct cage/
   proportional edits are a candidate method, not a guarantee of better art.

5. FUMO-REVIEW-LATENCY: reviews catch major defects late. Use one writer and
   one early image-only reviewer for the coarse study. Review against the
   references before revealing101 or the intended fix; compare old/new only
   afterward. Full two-reviewer final acceptance remains required. Avoid
   additional geometry-analysis workers unless one bounded fact changes the
   next modeling choice. Final104scalar-domain reviewer hit a usage limit;
   its missing result was explicitly disclosed, not invented or retried.

6. FUMO-RESUME-DRIFT: CURRENT retained historical future-tense instructions.
   Root separated active state from historical sections, but the next handoff
   should stay short: retained file, latest draft/verdict, exact next action.
   Save after each meaningful visual checkpoint. More receipts alone are not
   progress; no shared skills/policy/schema/host configuration are changed here.

## Credible alternatives and next falsifiable experiment

Continuing procedural patching has low asset-loss risk but poor demonstrated
visual return. Switching only to desktop/sculpt controls may help shape editing
but would not fix reference selection or late feedback. A fresh coarse whole
character study best tests the flawed foundation at low sunk cost.

Proposed next implementation milestone: one new disposable study, simple flat
colors and facial landmarks, no pile/stitches/dense remeshing/rig work. Model
head as a padded fabric cushion, hair as intentional broad cloth silhouettes,
sleeves as softly hanging forms, and the seated lower body as one coherent
arrangement. First compare front and the most revealing side/oblique view at
thumbnail and normal review sizes. Only if they improve the actual reference
relationships do the full fixed views and progressive sewn construction follow.

Success means recognizable overall silhouette/proportions and a convincing
plush read across views—not merely fewer contacts or a higher local score.
If this coarse study is not clearly closer, reject it before adding technical
detail and identify the missing reference or authoring capability. No guarantee
that a reset alone solves the artistic problem. No105study started in this
review; no criteria relaxed and no remote publication performed.
