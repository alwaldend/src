# Localized multi-brush sculpt capability coupon

This is one technique-only coupon on disposable geometry. It does not edit or
accept Reimu model bytes. The named sole Blender writer owns the live scene;
the coordinator and reviewers do not mutate it during the attempt.

## Causal distinction

Attempt 015 proved that broad Grab and proportional Edit transforms work, but
they could not independently flatten a pillow plane, seat a stem-like root, or
taper a free edge. This coupon tests the missing local mechanisms directly:
surface removal or flattening, controlled volume, smoothing, region isolation,
and multi-angle continuous strokes. More Grab strokes, renamed tools, arbitrary
strength changes, or another grid/Solidify/Bevel head assembly are forbidden.

## Frozen disposable fixture

Create one connected dense padded-panel fixture, separate from every Reimu
file. It deliberately contains a raised pillow center, a narrow stem root near
a locked receiver/contact witness, and a blunt free tip. Name and record the
root, plane, tip, and untouched control vertex regions. Freeze the fixture and
its coordinate digest before the first qualifying stroke.

Fixture construction may be deterministic because it is only a measurement
instrument. Every claimed corrective shape change must come from Blender's
native interactive sculpt path, not direct coordinate assignment, scripted
mesh replacement, or object-transform-only shaping.

## One complete coupon state

1. In the mapped Blender 5.1.1 loopback-MCP host, activate and read back the
   essential Grab, Smooth, Scrape or Flatten, and Inflate or Clay brush types.
2. Set and read back scene-locked radius and unified strength at two planned
   values. Record which value belongs to every stroke.
3. Establish mask or face-set isolation. For each operation, every named
   non-target control vertex must move no more than `1e-6` scene units.
4. From the fixed front view, deliver native press, at least six timer-spaced
   move events, and release for Scrape or Flatten. Reduce central pillow height
   or plane variance by at least 35 percent.
5. From the fixed three-quarter view, use controlled volume and Smooth strokes
   to increase root contact width by at least 25 percent and reduce root
   curvature discontinuity by at least 30 percent.
6. From the three-quarter view, use Grab and Smooth to taper the free tip to at
   most 0.55 of mid-panel width and reduce tip roughness by at least 25 percent.
7. Save the first complete passing-or-failing state to a new path. Then undo
   the complete geometry sequence natively and require the exact baseline
   coordinate digest in the live scene.
8. Clean-open the exact saved bytes with repository-pinned Blender 5.2.1,
   audit the recorded metrics and isolation, and render fixed front and
   three-quarter before/after views into new directories.

## Decision and stop rule

Pass only if all brush classes activate, radius and strength readback matches,
both view paths operate, isolation holds, all three shape metrics pass in the
same saved state, native undo is exact, and pinned clean-open/render succeeds.
Partial success fails.

Allow one correction only if the first state exposes a newly evidenced
transport or configuration mechanism and the changed input predicts a specific
observable repair. Otherwise stop after the first state. Failure blocks further
autonomous Reimu modeling pending a skilled Blender artist. A pass accepts only
the capability and authorizes exactly one fresh whole-head attempt; it does not
validate the four-form representation or any goal criterion.
