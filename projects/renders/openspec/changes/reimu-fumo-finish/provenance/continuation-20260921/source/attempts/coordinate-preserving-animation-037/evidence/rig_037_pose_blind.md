The triangle in the 37 side images is worth distinguishing from an invented
rig failure: it may be ordinary visibility through an existing neck/hair
clearance, and images alone cannot determine its cause. It is nevertheless
newly visible relative to neutral and prevents claiming that every contact
remains visually closed.

## Preexisting limitations, not posed regressions

SeatedNeutral_1 already contains the dark/scalloped under-head edge, stiff
hair/temple joins, a broad dark horizontal underside between the feet,
regular pointed hem, and awkward foot/hem overlaps. These persist through
the pose set; they should not be assigned to head yaw or arm motion without
additional evidence. Bow tail separation in side changes with yaw and is
consistent with revealing previously overlapping pieces.

ArmWave_13 is a small sleeve swing, not a convincing raised greeting pose.
That limits what this set proves about useful animation range. It is not
itself clipping, and it does not justify inventing a larger range requirement
for this review.

## Limits

Only the six supplied still poses and five cameras were inspected. No claim
is made about intervening frames, continuous playback, other joint angles,
hidden intersections, topology, rig mechanics, or file integrity. A useful
sampled motion check can coexist with unresolved base-model fidelity and
the small newly exposed neck opening documented above.
