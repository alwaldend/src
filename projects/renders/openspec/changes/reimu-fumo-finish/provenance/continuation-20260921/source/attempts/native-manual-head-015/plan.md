# Native manual head-and-hair clay reset

The sole model writer is the agent identified as `native_head_artist`. The
coordinator and reviewers must not edit Blender model bytes during this
attempt.

## Frozen input and new capability

Start from protected A157, SHA-256
`433d08ad36be488bb16e4221a85f831d4390660c258a43ea0b08775811574b73`.
Do not use candidates 013 or 014 as model input. Preserve the body, costume,
bow, face-layout donor, cameras, lights, and protected source bytes.

Procedural panel boxes, continuous replacement shells, integrated unshaped
substrates, and analytic radial lobes are retired. This plan is permitted only
because `macro-sculpt-010` proved timer-spaced native Grab control, save,
clean-reopen, pinned render, and undo. The falsifiable difference is manual
multi-view deformation of organic stock rather than another coordinate
formula or scalar cut.

## Diagnosed target

The A157 cap covers 81.4 percent of the head at an almost uniform sub-mm
offset. Its total depth is already in band, but crown, sides, and rear collapse
into one capsule. The front is also vertically compressed: lock-excluded head
height is about `0.83 Wh` instead of `0.986 Wh`, and crown-to-lowest-lock reach
is about `0.906 Wh` instead of `1.098 Wh`.

## One complete manual state

1. Copy protected A157 to a fresh working path and verify its hash. Bake the
   evaluated lattice state of `Head_Cushion_Manual_Target` through Blender's
   native modifier operator before sculpting it. Hide the old continuous cap
   and obsolete old hair construction; do not delete donor objects.
2. Use native Sculpt/Edit operators to form one shallow gusseted head cushion
   with a readable front plane, softly rounded crown transition, and tapered
   nape. Direct vertex-coordinate assignment is forbidden.
3. Create and manually shape at most four independent hair forms: one
   crown/rear drape, left and right side/forelock forms, and one central fringe.
   They must be rooted, thinly padded, and overlapping. Simple stock may be
   added through Blender operators, but formulas, generated radial lobes,
   enclosing second skins, object-transform-only final forms, and card walls
   are forbidden.
4. Use the existing A157 brown and beige clay materials. Preserve the existing
   eyes and mouth as layout witnesses; move them only if a native grouped
   transform is required to keep them seated on the face plane.
5. Save the first complete whole-head state once to a new candidate. Do not
   render and then correct it. The coordinator will clean-open and render it
   in repository-pinned Blender 5.2.1.

## Fast-trio gate

The fixed front, side, and most revealing three-quarter views must
simultaneously show a shallow face cushion, non-helmet crown transition,
tapered nape, independently rooted lower locks, and full side/rear coverage.
The measurement targets are:

- lock-excluded head height `0.986 Wh +/- 0.03 Wh`;
- crown-to-lowest-lock reach `1.098 Wh +/- 0.05 Wh`;
- visible face width and height `0.603 Wh +/- 0.03 Wh`;
- central fringe position within `0.03 Wh`;
- head depth `0.66-0.82 Wh` and outer hair depth `0.71-0.87 Wh`;
- upper width about `0.39 Wh` at `0.05 Wh` below the crown and `0.57 Wh` at
  `0.10 Wh`, widening to about `0.89 Wh` at `0.30 Wh`; and
- lower envelope at least `0.95 Wh` wide near `0.80 Wh` below the crown.

Reset without correction on any helmet, rounded box, card, pillow cluster,
pale gap, floating root, crossing, or tangency above `0.02 Wh`, or any frozen
body, bow, or camera change. Retention requires implementation-blind review.

If this first complete manual state fails an automatic representation gate,
the current broad-Grab/native-transform toolset is insufficient for this
rebuild. Stop autonomous model variants and require a skilled Blender artist
or a separately proven richer native sculpt toolset.
