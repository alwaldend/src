## Construction decision

**Revise the rest pattern.** The canonical front shows a relatively smooth
red lap panel with a separately gathered white hem. Exact-variant turn frames
12 and 16 show the side and rear cloth approaching the support plane. The
physical side photograph supports that layer order. Study 117 instead placed
short, heavily gathered cloth above an exposed seated body.

The independent reference review found no evidence for shrinking the hidden
pelvis. Preserve its envelope for this test. From a waist at 56 mm high and
approximately 33.5 by 27.5 mm radii, the proposed hem landmarks imply minimum
straight surface spans of about 40 mm front, 56 mm side and 68 mm rear, before
contact detours or folds. Study 117's uniform 58 mm length cannot supply the
rear path. These are construction measurements, not accepted geometry.

Test a flared annular-sector or equivalent joined-panel rest pattern with
modest waist gathering and greater rear reach. A roughly half-circle flare
adds about 226 mm of hem circumference over a 72 mm slant length; with the
current waist, the resulting 430–450 mm perimeter is consistent with the
required hem envelope. The actual Fumo sewing pattern is unknown. This is an
explicit modeling inference supported by the patternmaker's
[circle-skirt construction](https://dresspatternmaking.com/patternmaking-basics/garment-elements/skirts-e/circle-skirts).

The credible alternative is a longer rectangular gathered skirt, documented
in the [doll dressmaking tutorial](https://www.phoebeandegg.com/blog/2014/9/16/doll-dressmaking-seriesdress-with-a-bodice-and-gathered-skirt).
It preserves the strong waist compression that caused 117's bunching, so it
is not selected for this test. White trim must be an independent strip,
approximately 7–10 mm finished width, with fullness at its free edge; merely
recoloring the last rows was visibly insufficient. The initial 1.5–2 gather
ratio is a sewing hypothesis, not an exact-variant measurement; see the
[maker's ruffle construction](https://www.inclusivesewing.com/blog/add-ruffle-hem-easy-sewing-tutorial).

## Execution and result

The root wrapper ran in pinned Blender 5.2.1 LTS `9e2066aef7ef`. All 120
frames completed in 34.42 seconds. The final ten-frame maximum displacement
was 2.49 mm per frame, so the cloth did not settle. The actual contact paths
were 38.90 mm front, 54.90 mm sides and 67.13 mm rear; the corresponding rest
lengths were 46, 61 and 74 mm. No native setup repair was needed. Root reduced
the draft's wall deadline to the recorded 720 seconds before execution.

Saved candidate `assembly_118_pattern.blend`, SHA-256
`9cfe4cb84c8607dea88898dc9de17c50a712488e8a38c3e606110731a1e1475b`,
clean-reopened and rendered all four fixed views. Protected source bytes were
unchanged; the candidate has no cloth modifiers or live-cache dependency.
Local receipts, rest edges, frame records, frozen coordinates, measurements
and pixels are under `out/reimu_fumo_finish/assembly_118/`.

**Root rejects the garment module.** The solver pulled the front edge back
from the feet and left most of the red seat exposed. The back became a
displaced flap. Total evaluated width is 142.13 mm, but depth is only 98.69 mm
against 131.02–152.20 mm. The 2.01 mm minimum height reaches the floor at an
isolated fold, not a correctly seated whole hem. These results demonstrate
why extrema alone cannot establish shape. The white strip has an authored
free-to-sewn edge ratio of 2.005 but inherits the wrong parent boundary and
receives no separate acceptance.

Implementation-blind reviewer `/root/pattern118_blind` independently rejects
the same frozen views: likeness 6, proportions 6, construction 4, identity 8,
contacts 5, cloth read 4 and presentation 6 out of 10. Its highest-impact
defects are the raised, bunched skirt and exposed lower body, blocklike hair,
hollow rigid sleeves, excessive leg exposure and stiff bow. Root agrees.

This hypothesis is closed rejected. Do not carry this garment
into study 119 or run another physical pattern/settings sweep. Any later
cloth investigation first needs a small calibration fixture proving that the
chosen numerical material can retain a known seated drape at 25 cm scale.
Meanwhile the connected-hair study uses preserved 116 C context. Outcome and
execution remain open and active; no modeling tier advances.
