## Decision and evidence

**Replace the representation.** Independent inspection of the canonical turn
frames, physical side photograph and frozen 117 views identifies three bulky
rear blocks, a transverse side belt and a pale temple wedge. Reference hair
has long, comparatively thin overlapping panels continuing from the crown
into angled free hems. Current rear panels have independently inflated closed
volumes, approximately 5.7 mm thick centrally, overlapping an additional
2.8 mm side sheet. Earlier root-fitting repairs did not fix that topology.

Preserve the traced front crown and bang constraints. Replace the rear and
side assembly with one connected midsurface above its slit roots, continuing
into three unequal rear lobes and two side continuations. Duplicate edges
only below the slit roots so adjacent ends can overlap in a consistent order.
Use shallow normal-direction thickness, initially 1–2 mm, instead of separate
world-Y inflated volumes. Keep any backing fitted directly to the head and
above the free ends; it must not create another visible lower belt. The
pattern is a modeling inference from photographs, not a recovered sewing
pattern. Narrow wrapped bands and flatter cheek locks may be included in the
same coherent surface-construction change if their reference constraints are
explicitly preserved.

The credible alternative is separately bent two-dimensional felt panels
attached at crown seams. It permits literal lap seams but retains more
independent root and collision boundaries. Select the connected upper shell
because it directly removes the repeated attachment and wedge failures.

### Recorded causal geometry repairs

1. The continuation starts too far behind the cheek lock, leaving a visible
   pale strip. Extend its two front angular boundaries from columns 34/158 to
   28/164 while preserving all original cap control points. Predicted effect:
   the side continuation covers the attachment gap behind the lock without
   moving the crown or central fringe. Check front-face occlusion explicitly.

   curves with unequal, off-center angled endpoints inside the same thin

   overlapping ends instead of equal rounded lobes. Preserve crown bounds,
   head clearance and total depth. Do not add thickness or a side belt.
