## Decision

**Proceed with one bounded macro scaffold.** A physical solver is not required
to establish the already observed garment envelope. Reconstruct one continuous
waist-to-hem midsurface from canonical front and turntable landmarks, checking
every angular strip against evaluated body and foot support. This introduces
reference and contact constraints missing from 116's independently authored
lap, sides and rear. Merely joining its five meshes would not be a reset.

Independent decision review agrees that correcting known macro coverage need
not wait for a physical-material fixture. The credible alternative is to
calibrate the solver first; it remains necessary before relying on its fabric
behavior. This scaffold explicitly makes no simulated-drape, sewn-construction
or final cloth claim. It cannot advance a modeling tier by itself.

The front hem should extend to approximately Y=-66 mm and Z=28–33 mm,
clearing the actual foot tops. Keep side hem near X=±61 mm and Z=25 mm until
the rear quadrant; descend behind the feet toward the floor, with a narrower
rear region near Y=+60 mm and Z=3 mm. The separate gathered white strip is
approximately 9 mm wide and must itself clear support surfaces. Target complete
hem width is 140.95 mm and depth is 131.02–152.20 mm. The upper waist remains
near Z=56 mm. Preserve the hidden body rather than shrinking it to mask gaps.

The main regression risks are another rigid front shelf, a rear cape, and low
rear trim protruding into the front silhouette. A continuous mesh does not
prove that these defects are fixed. Review front, side, rear and three-quarter
pixels and the calibrated overlay before any bounded retention decision.

## Execution limits

Select and record a frozen assembly source after the 119 result. Preserve all
source bytes, head, facial guides, bow and sleeves. Replace the skirt surfaces
and trim only. Use the existing pinned background Blender route and four
threads. Build one candidate with the reference-conditioned boundary and
evaluated support clearance; permit at most one diagnosed contact or topology
setup repair. Do not tune appearance after the frozen review within this study.
Stop on invalid geometry, nonfinite coordinates, visible penetration or a
failed review. Save new bytes and render from a clean reopen. Do not add
textures, fibers, microdetail, rigging or animation.
