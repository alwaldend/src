# Add advisory planning, admission, and reusable evidence

## Why

The accepted Phase 3 work introduced advisory plans, explicit action admission, and reusable candidate-bound evidence so execution and acceptance could share precise identities without sharing mutation ownership.

This change was imported as completed historical work on 2026-09-08. The source outcome is `achieved` and execution is `paused`. Checked tasks reproduce its accepted review; they are not a new validation of current code. Historical deltas were not applied to the independently documented current baseline.

## What Changes

- Deterministic advisory planning: ImpactPlan resolves profiles, capabilities, allowed and forbidden effects, narrow changed and reverse-affected targets, minimum checks, coverage gaps, cost, escalation, and applicable evidence from canonical inputs.
- Admission at provider boundaries: Read and hermetic computation retain a cheap path. Remote effects follow prepare, validate, authorize, execute, and verify, with refusal on stale state, wrong environment, missing budgets, and unknown contracts.
- Immutable validation and separate assertions: ValidationSet binds candidate, checks, arguments, scope, toolchain and policy identities, result, coverage, bounds, and limitations. EvidenceAssertion applies immutable validation to a criterion revision; tree-bound and commit-bound applicability remain distinct.
- Resumable structured audits: Full-repository reports are incremental JSON with generated Markdown, selectors, exact input binding, target counts, and resume only on identical inputs. Raw logs remain task-local and bounded.
